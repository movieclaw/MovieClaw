## iPhone and iPad 0.12.7: Find what you have not finished

Library home rows and poster walls can now distinguish titles you have not started from titles you started but have not finished. Opening **View All** keeps the row's watch condition and sorting instead of showing a different list.

> ✅ Works with MovieClaw Server 0.28.0 or later, the same as 0.12.6. The new **Unfinished** condition requires Server 0.47.0 or later. This build is the unsigned IPA at https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/tag/ios-v0.12.7. It is not on TestFlight.

### Highlights

#### Continue from the titles that still have something left

Choose **Unfinished** while customizing a library home row to include movies you have not finished and series with available episodes left to watch. **Unwatched** remains a separate choice for titles you have never started. Cross-library poster walls also let you switch among **All**, **Unwatched**, **Unfinished**, and **Watched**; if a condition finds nothing, one tap clears it.

### Fixed

- Switching the watch condition or sorting quickly no longer lets a slower response for the previous choice replace the current poster wall.

Thanks to @o1xhack. Full changes: [ios-v0.12.6 → ios-v0.12.7](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/ios-v0.12.6...ios-v0.12.7).

## 简体中文

## iPhone 与 iPad 0.12.7：找出还没看完的作品

资料库首页行和海报墙现在能区分「从没开始看」与「已经开始、但还没看完」。从首页点「查看全部」时，也会保留这一行的观看条件和排序，不会打开另一份列表。

> ✅ 搭配 MovieClaw 服务器 0.28.0 或更新版本即可使用，与 0.12.6 相同。新的「未看完」条件需要服务器 0.47.0 或更新版本。这一版是未签名 IPA：https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/tag/ios-v0.12.7。没有上传到 TestFlight。

### 本版亮点

#### 从还有内容没看完的作品继续

自定义资料库首页行时选择「未看完」，会包含还没看完的电影，以及仍有在架集数没看完的剧集。「未看」仍是另一项，只包含从未开始的作品。跨库海报墙也能在「全部」「未看」「未看完」「已看」之间切换；筛选后没有结果时，可以一键清除条件。

### 修好了

- 快速切换观看条件或排序时，较慢返回的旧请求不会再把海报墙换回上一个条件。

感谢 @o1xhack。完整改动：[ios-v0.12.6 → ios-v0.12.7](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/ios-v0.12.6...ios-v0.12.7)。

## v0.30.0: Skip the intro, roll into the next episode

Binge-watching gets easier: TV episodes can skip their intro, and recognized end credits roll straight into the next episode. The web player keeps your quality and recovers from network drops by itself, and the Library page can combine several libraries of the same type into one row.

> ✅ No Docker image update is required. Update from Settings → Update & Maintenance.

### Highlights

#### No more dragging past the same opening

Watching a season back to back meant scrubbing past the same intro every episode. Now MovieClaw compares the audio across a season to find the intro, sponsor spots before it and the end credits, and shows a **Skip intro (跳过片头)** button. When credits that run to the end begin, the **Playing next (即将播放)** card counts down 8 seconds and plays the next episode. It's on for every TV library after the update, in the web player and in iPhone app 0.3.0 or later.

- Tap ✕ on the card to keep watching the credits. Auto-play stops after three episodes in a row with nobody touching the player.
- Credits followed by more of the episode get **Skip credits (跳过片尾)** instead of the card.
- Nothing is found for seasons with fewer than three episodes in the library, intros shorter than 15 seconds or outside the first 10 minutes, or credits whose music changes every episode; the card then appears only in the last 40 seconds, without a countdown.
- New episodes are analyzed as they arrive. Existing shows are filled in in the background, the ones you're watching first, so older shows get the button later.
- Jellyfin clients such as Infuse may also show a skip button.

#### The web player keeps your quality and reconnects by itself

On a slow line the web player used to switch to a lower-quality transcode on its own, and a dropped connection cost you quality too. Now it reconnects at the same quality and picks up where you were; when the line really can't keep up, a card shows the measured speed and offers a lower quality, and the choice is yours. A file that is gone from the server now says **File not found on the server (服务器上找不到这个文件)** instead of retrying. It applies after the update, nothing to set up.

- The quality you pick is remembered per title. The single quality setting from earlier versions is reset to Auto.
- When a title opens with a non-default quality, audio track or subtitle from last time, a short notice tells you.
- The first play of some MP4 files and large TS files no longer waits while the server reads the whole file; in our NAS tests that took a minute or more.
- In Safari on iPhone and iPad, HEVC videos now play through the system player, which may stop seeking and Skip intro from stalling for a second or two.

#### All your movies in one row

With movies spread over several libraries, the Library page showed one row per library and the same film could appear twice. Now **All movies (全部电影)**, **All TV shows (全部剧集)** and **All other videos (全部其他视频)** rows combine the libraries of one type, each title once; open the row title to browse them all. A row appears by itself when you have two or more libraries of that type, and you can add one with **Customize home (自定义首页)** at the top right of the Library page (in its ⋯ menu on phones) → **+ Add row (＋ 添加一行 · 从哪来？)**. These rows are web only for now; the iPhone app doesn't show them.

- Known issue: saving Customize home in the iPhone app deletes the rows you added on the web and resets the rest to their defaults. Fixed in v0.31.0 (iPhone app 0.4.0); update to it.

### New features

- **GitHub access token**: If checking for updates reports GitHub rate limiting, add a token in Settings → Update & Maintenance → **GitHub access token (GitHub 访问令牌)**; a fine-grained token with no permissions is enough, or use `mclaw app update github-token-put`.
- **Resume bar on iPhone**: The bar above the iPhone app's tab bar shows only what you played on this iPhone, not titles from Infuse or other devices. The **Up next (接下来继续)** row on the Library page still covers all devices.
- **New logo**: The iPhone app icon (with dark and tinted versions), the web app, the Mac transcoder and the browser extension have a new logo, and browser tabs now show it too.
- **Clips (片段) on iPhone**: The filter menu gains **Other (其他)** for "other video" libraries, though Clips still draws only from movies and TV shows by default. The page gains a back button, and its title opens the details page. Swiping sideways on a clip in portrait no longer seeks; seek in full screen instead.

### Improvements

- **Faster remote transcoding on a Mac**: With this release's Mac transcoder, starting and seeking in a transcoded title is faster, most of all for Blu-ray discs and TS files. Download https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.30.0/MovieClawTranscoder-macos-arm64.zip and replace the old app; the old one keeps working as before.

### Fixed

- Watching the longer version of a title with several versions, such as an extended cut, could mark it watched well before the end and lose the resume point.
- In the web player, picking a lower quality from the quality menu before the picture appeared did nothing.
- While a library scan ran alongside a metadata refresh that read local NFO files, some new files failed with "database is locked" and were left out of that scan.
- In Settings → Update & Maintenance → Cache Management, **Clean up orphaned entries (清理孤儿条目)** failed with an error.
- And 1 more fix for Jellyfin clients.

### For administrators

- After the update, every TV library is analyzed in the background: the first 10 and last 7 minutes of each episode are read, using disk and CPU, and the work pauses while anyone is watching. Progress shows on the **Activity (活动)** page; to turn it off for a library, open it → ⋯ → **Edit library (编辑库)** → **Detect intros and credits (识别片头片尾)**, keeping the intros already found.
- The analysis keeps small fingerprint files, listed in Cache Management as **Intro and credits fingerprints (片头片尾指纹)**. Clearing them keeps the intros already found, but the next new episode of a season makes the whole season be read again.
- Installs from source need an ffmpeg with chromaprint, such as jellyfin-ffmpeg, or nothing is detected. The Docker image already has it.
- Shared collections (not set to **Only me (只有我可见)**) can now be changed only by their creator and administrators. Existing ones are assigned to the administrator, so members can no longer rename, edit or delete them, even ones they made.
- In member permissions, **Site search (站点搜索)** is now **Resource search (资源搜索)**. Members without it still get the search button, for movie and TV info if they have **Subscriptions (订阅追踪)**, and for their visible libraries.
- Members now see download progress on subscriptions they started or follow, without torrent or downloader names.
- Members with Subscriptions, Resource search and **One-click download (一键下载)** can now use **Pick torrent manually (手动选种)** on subscriptions they started; when they download, the save-location dialog lists only their visible libraries and the downloader's default folder.

### Before you update

- The iPhone features above need iPhone app 0.3.0: download https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.30.0/MovieClaw-iOS-unsigned.ipa and install it over the old app the same way you installed it.
- This release migrates the database. Updating from Settings → Update & Maintenance makes an automatic backup first. To return to v0.29.0, use **Choose a version to roll back to… (选择版本回退…)** on the same page; it restores that backup, so anything recorded after the update is lost.
- If you update by pulling the Docker image instead, no backup is made: stop the container and back up the data directory first. To return, stop the container, change the image tag to `movieclaw/movieclaw:v0.29.0`, restore that backup, then start it.

Full changes: [v0.29.0 → v0.30.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.29.0...v0.30.0).

## 简体中文

## v0.30.0：追剧不用再拖片头

追剧更省事了：剧集能一键跳过片头，认出片尾后自动接下一集。网页播放器会守住你选的画质、断线自己重连，「媒体库」页还能把同类型的几个库合成一行。

> ✅ 本版无需更新 Docker 镜像，在「设置 → 更新与维护」一键更新即可。

### 本版亮点

#### 不用每集都拖过同一段片头

连着刷一季剧，每集都要手动拖过同一段片头。现在 movieclaw 会把同一季各集的声音互相比对，认出片头、片头前的冠名广告和片尾，播到时出现「跳过片头」按钮。一直放到结尾的片尾一开始，就弹出「即将播放」卡片，倒计时 8 秒自动放下一集。升级后所有剧集库自动开启，网页播放器和 iPhone App 0.3.0 或更新版本都能用。

- 点卡片右上角的 ✕ 可以继续看片尾。连续自动播了三集、期间没人碰过播放器，就不再自动播。
- 片尾之后还有正片内容的，给的是「跳过片尾」按钮，不弹卡片。
- 以下情况认不出：库里这一季不到三集、片头短于 15 秒或不在前 10 分钟内、每集片尾配乐都不同；这时卡片只在最后 40 秒出现，也不倒计时。
- 新集入库时顺手识别。已有的剧在后台慢慢补，正在追的排在最前，老剧要过一阵才有按钮。
- Infuse 等 Jellyfin 客户端可能也会出现跳过按钮。

#### 网页播放器守住画质，断线自己接上

以前网页播放器在网速慢时会自作主张改成转码、降低画质，断一次线也要降一档。现在断线后按原画质自动重连、从刚才的位置接着放；线路确实跟不上时，弹出卡片显示实测速度、建议换低一档，换不换由你。文件已经不在服务器上时，会直接提示「服务器上找不到这个文件」，不会反复重试。升级后自动生效，不用设置。

- 画质按每部片分别记住。以前版本里那个对所有片生效的画质设置会恢复成「自动」。
- 打开一部片时沿用了上次的非默认画质、音轨或字幕，会短暂提示一下。
- 部分 MP4 和大体积 TS 文件第一次播放时，不用再等服务器把整个文件读一遍；在我们的 NAS 测试中，这一步原来要一分钟以上。
- iPhone 和 iPad 的 Safari 里，HEVC 视频改用系统播放器，拖进度、跳过片头可能不会再停顿一两秒。

#### 几个库的电影，合成一行

电影分在好几个库里，「媒体库」页只能一个库一行地翻，同一部片还可能出现两次。现在「全部电影」「全部剧集」「全部其他视频」把同类型的库合成一行，同一部片只出现一次；点行标题可以浏览全部。同类型有两个及以上的库时自动出现；要自己加，点「媒体库」页右上角的「自定义首页」（手机上在 ⋯ 菜单里）→「＋ 添加一行 · 从哪来？」。目前只有网页有这几行，iPhone App 里不显示。

- 已知问题：在 iPhone App 里保存「自定义首页」，会删掉你在网页上加的这几行，其余的恢复成默认设置。已在 v0.31.0（iPhone App 0.4.0）修复，升级即可。

### 新功能

- **GitHub 访问令牌**：检查更新时提示 GitHub 限流，可以在「设置 → 更新与维护 → GitHub 访问令牌」里填一个令牌，不勾选任何权限的 Fine-grained token 即可；也可以用 `mclaw app update github-token-put` 设置。
- **iPhone 上的接着看条**：App 标签栏上方的接着看条只显示在这台 iPhone 上播过的片，不会混进 Infuse 或其他设备上看的。「媒体库」页的「接下来继续」仍然跨设备。
- **新标志**：iPhone App 图标（含深色与着色版本）、网页、Mac 转码器、浏览器扩展都换上了新标志，浏览器标签页也有了图标。
- **iPhone 的「片段」页**：筛选菜单多了「其他」，用来刷「其他视频」库，默认仍只从电影和剧集里抽。页面加了返回键，点顶部的片名进详情页。竖屏时横滑不会再拖进度，要拖进度请进全屏。

### 体验改进

- **Mac 远程转码更快**：换上这一版的 Mac 转码器后，转码播放的起播和拖进度更快，原盘和 TS 文件最明显。从 https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.30.0/MovieClawTranscoder-macos-arm64.zip 下载后替换旧版；不更新也照常能用。

### 修好了

- 多版本影片看较长的那一版（比如加长版）时，离结尾还远就被标成已看、续播点丢失的问题已修复。
- 网页播放器里，画面出来之前从画质菜单选低画质没有反应的问题已修复。
- 扫描媒体库的同时刷新元数据、读取本地 NFO 时，部分新文件报「database is locked」、这次扫描入不了库的问题已修复。
- 「设置 → 更新与维护 → 缓存管理」里点「清理孤儿条目」报错的问题已修复。
- 以及 1 处 Jellyfin 客户端相关的修复。

### 管理员须知

- 升级后所有剧集库会在后台识别片头片尾：每集读开头 10 分钟和结尾 7 分钟，会占用读盘和 CPU，有人在看片时自动暂停。进度在「活动」页查看；要关掉某个库的识别，进该库 → ⋯ →「编辑库」，关掉「识别片头片尾」，已认出的结果会保留。
- 识别会留下很小的指纹文件，在「缓存管理」里显示为「片头片尾指纹」。清空后已认出的片头片尾不受影响，但之后这一季再来新集时，要把整季重新读一遍。
- 源码部署需要带 chromaprint 的 ffmpeg（如 jellyfin-ffmpeg），否则识别不出来。Docker 镜像已自带。
- 全家共享的合集（没选「只有我可见」的）现在只有创建者和管理员能改。已有的这类合集一律归管理员，成员不能再改名、编辑或删除，包括他们自己建的。
- 成员权限里的「站点搜索」更名为「资源搜索」。没有这项权限的成员也会看到搜索入口：有「订阅追踪」权限的可以搜影视，也能搜自己可见的媒体库。
- 成员现在能在自己发起或关注的订阅里看到下载进度，但看不到种子名和下载器名。
- 同时有「订阅追踪」「资源搜索」「一键下载」权限的成员，现在可以在自己发起的订阅里「手动选种」；他们下载时，保存位置弹窗只列出自己可见的媒体库和下载器默认目录。

### 升级须知

- 上面提到的 iPhone 功能需要 iPhone App 0.3.0：下载 https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.30.0/MovieClaw-iOS-unsigned.ipa，用你当初安装的方式覆盖安装旧版。
- 本版包含数据库迁移。在「设置 → 更新与维护」更新时会先自动备份。要回到 v0.29.0，在同一页点「选择版本回退…」，它会恢复这份备份，更新之后产生的数据会丢失。
- 如果你是拉取 Docker 镜像升级的，不会自动备份：先停止容器并备份数据目录。要回退时停止容器，把镜像标签改回 `movieclaw/movieclaw:v0.29.0`，恢复这份备份后再启动。

完整变更：[v0.29.0 → v0.30.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.29.0...v0.30.0)。

## iPhone and iPad 0.13.0: Pick another episode without leaving playback

Series now have an episode picker inside the player. You can see where you are, move to another available episode, or change seasons without returning to the detail page.

> ✅ Works with MovieClaw Server 0.28.0 or later, the same as 0.12.7. Changing seasons inside the player requires Server 0.50.0 or later; older supported servers still show the current season. This build is the unsigned IPA at https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/tag/ios-v0.13.0. It is not uploaded to TestFlight.

### Highlights

#### Keep the picture in sight while choosing an episode

While a series is playing, tap **Episodes** or the **Next episode** capsule in the bottom controls. The picker opens beside or below the picture, centers the episode currently playing, shows watch progress, and leaves missing episodes visible but unavailable. Other episodes do not reveal their synopsis before you play them.

### Fixed

- On the iPhone Duo vertical tab bar, long-pressing or double-tapping the avatar works reliably, the gesture tip stays beside the avatar, and action labels remain readable.
- Item details stay within narrow regular-width windows instead of extending past the visible stage.

Full changes: [ios-v0.12.7 → ios-v0.13.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/ios-v0.12.7...ios-v0.13.0).

## 简体中文

## iPhone 与 iPad 0.13.0：不用退出播放就能换一集

剧集播放器现在自带选集。你可以直接看见正在播放的位置，切换到另一集，也能换季，不必先退回详情页。

> ✅ 搭配 MovieClaw 服务器 0.28.0 或更新版本即可使用，与 0.12.7 相同。在播放器里跨季切换需要服务器 0.50.0 或更新版本；更早但仍受支持的服务器仍会显示当前季。这一版是未签名 IPA：https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/tag/ios-v0.13.0。没有上传到 TestFlight。

### 本版亮点

#### 画面还在眼前，就能挑下一集

播放剧集时，点底栏的「选集」或「下一集」胶囊。选集面板会出现在画面旁边或下方，自动把正在播放的一集放到中间，显示观看进度；缺集仍会列出，但不能点。还没播放的其他集不会提前显示简介。

### 修好了

- iPhone Duo 使用竖向标签栏时，长按或双击头像现在能稳定触发；手势提示会留在头像旁边，操作文字也能完整显示。
- 窄的 regular-width 窗口中，条目详情不会再伸出可见舞台。

完整改动：[ios-v0.12.7 → ios-v0.13.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/ios-v0.12.7...ios-v0.13.0)。

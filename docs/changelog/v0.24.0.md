## v0.24.0: Put series of films together, and know which episode comes next

Automatic, manual and cross-library collections join a Continue Watching home row. Detail pages need less disk access on NAS devices, with fixes for stalled subscriptions and quality upgrades.

✅ If you already use the image for v0.23.0, no Docker image update is required. Update from **Settings → Update & Maintenance**.

### Highlights

#### Film series group themselves, or make your own watchlist

Related films form collections automatically and show missing titles, with existing libraries filled in in the background. Build manual or cross-library collections, reorder and share them, or hide and restore collections you do not want displayed.

#### Pick up at the next episode you should watch

Continue Watching starts from your viewing position and finds the next unfinished episode whose file remains available. Unfinished favorites come first, and playback no longer changes when a title was favorited.

#### Move a batch when combining libraries or changing disks

The backend and mclaw support batch transfers and consolidating library roots. A preflight checks space, same-disk versus cross-disk costs and naming conflicts. Resume after failures; source roots stay registered until their files have been moved.

### More features and improvements

- **Less repeated NAS disk access**: Cache subtitles and artwork by directory and absorb NFO data on import instead of parsing it on every detail request.
- **More complete subscription searches**: Search English, Chinese and original titles instead of stopping after the first query returns something.
- **Optional timeline previews**: Turn generation off in Settings → Playback; already generated previews remain usable.
- **Playback follows content ratings**: Direct URLs cannot bypass content restrictions that hide a title from a member's lists.
- **More responsive seeking**: Less unnecessary rendering during frequent drags, with the handle kept inside the controls.

### Fixed

- Episode names and descriptions are no longer frozen by old NFO files written by MovieClaw; user-provided NFO files are not silently overwritten or ignored.
- Implausibly small fake feature films are rejected during automatic selection instead of downloading trailers as full movies.
- Re-subscribing to the same torrent after deleting a title no longer remains stuck at Delivered.
- Individual episode torrents sharing a directory retain the right sources; stalled quality-upgrade tasks finish in later checks.
- Long seeks keep the timeline and time readout aligned, with more accurate transfer speed readings.
- Concurrent AI replies no longer keep shuffling the recent-conversations sidebar.

### Before you update

- Batch transfers and root consolidation are currently available through the backend and mclaw, without a web interface.
- This release migrates the database. Restore the automatic pre-update backup when rolling back across versions.

Full changes: [v0.23.0 → v0.24.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.23.0...v0.24.0).

## 简体中文

## v0.24.0：系列电影聚成合集，追剧知道接着看哪集

这一版带来自动、手动与跨库合集，首页改为「接下来继续」。NAS 上浏览详情更省读盘，订阅和洗版卡住的问题也修了一批。

✅ 已使用 v0.23.0 对应镜像的用户无需更新 Docker 镜像，在「设置 → 更新与维护」一键更新即可。

### 本版亮点

#### 系列电影自动聚在一起，也能自己建片单

同系列作品会自动组成合集，并显示还缺哪些片；旧片库会在后台补齐。也能手动挑片、跨库组合集、排序和分享，不想显示的合集可以隐藏再恢复。

#### 下一次开播，直接找到该看的那集

首页「接下来继续」从观看位置出发，找出文件仍在、还没看完的下一集。收藏里没看完的排在前面，播放也不再改变收藏时间。

#### 换盘、合并库，一次搬一批

后端和 mclaw 支持批量转移、合并媒体库根路径。先预检空间、同盘与跨盘代价和重名冲突，失败后可以从检查点继续；源目录没搬干净前不会移除原根路径。

### 其他新功能与改进

- **NAS 浏览少反复读盘**：字幕、图片按目录缓存，NFO 在入库时吸收，打开详情不再重复解析磁盘文件。
- **订阅搜索更完整**：同时用英文名、中文名和原名查找，避免第一个词有结果就停止。
- **预览图生成可关闭**：「设置 → 播放」新增进度条预览开关，已生成的图片仍可使用。
- **成员分级覆盖播放**：列表中受限制的内容，拿到直链也不能绕过限制播放。
- **拖进度更跟手**：高频拖动减少多余绘制，进度把手不再超出控制条边缘。

### 修好了

- 分集名称和简介不再被自己写出的旧 NFO 冻结，用户提供的 NFO 也不会被静默覆盖或忽略。
- 体积离谱的假正片会在自动选种时拦下，避免把预告片当整部电影下载。
- 删除后重新订阅同一个种子，不再永远卡在「已投递」。
- 同目录的单集种子不会再错记来源，已经卡住的洗版任务会在后续巡检中收尾。
- 远距离跳转后，进度条和时间不再各自显示不同位置，取流速度读数也更准确。
- 多个 AI 会话同时输出时，侧栏不再反复抢着重排。

### 升级须知

- 批量转移与根路径归并目前通过后端和 mclaw 使用，网页尚未提供入口。
- 本版升级了数据库。如需跨版本回退，请用更新前自动生成的备份恢复。

完整改动见 [v0.23.0 → v0.24.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.23.0...v0.24.0)。

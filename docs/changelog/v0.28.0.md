## v0.28.0: MovieClaw on your iPhone

The first native iPhone app arrives: browse, subscribe and play your library on iPhone, including Blu-ray folders and ISO images. The Mac transcoder is rebuilt around a status panel and can convert Blu-ray discs, and a new Devices page shows everywhere your account is signed in.

> ✅ No Docker image update is required. Update from Settings → Update & Maintenance.
> ⚠️ **iPhone app**: it needs this server version; older servers refuse sign-in from the app.
> ⚠️ **If you use the Mac transcoder**: download [MovieClawTranscoder-macos-arm64.zip](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.28.0/MovieClawTranscoder-macos-arm64.zip) and replace the old app. It is now called MovieClaw 转码器; delete the old MovieClaw Transcoder.app so you don't end up with two (settings and pairing carry over). The old version keeps working, but Blu-ray and Infuse transcodes are not sent to it; the server transcodes them itself or plays the original.

### Highlights

#### Your library as a real iPhone app

On iPhone you could only open the web page, where large files and discs often needed transcoding or didn't play. The new app plays files straight from your server, including 4K HDR, Blu-ray folders, DVD and ISO, with picture-in-picture and AirPlay. Download [MovieClaw-iOS-unsigned.ipa](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.28.0/MovieClaw-iOS-unsigned.ipa) and install it with AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly using your own Apple ID.

- Needs iOS 26 or later. It is made for iPhone (iPad runs it in compatibility mode), and the interface is Chinese only.
- Sign in with your server address, user name and password; on first launch it looks for servers on your local network. You can keep several servers and accounts.
- Subscription rules, resource sites, downloaders and auto-import are set up on the web; the app sends you there.
- An app signed with a free Apple ID expires after 7 days unless AltStore or SideStore renews it.

#### A Mac transcoder you can just open

The Mac transcoder used to be a menu bar item with a settings window; it is now a status panel showing which title it is converting. It starts at login and restarts its converter by itself after a crash. It can convert Blu-ray folders and turns HDR and Dolby Vision into SDR on the GPU. The download is notarized by Apple, so it opens with a double-click.

- Apple Silicon Macs, macOS 12 or later. ISO images and DVDs can't be converted yet.
- GPU HDR conversion needs Jellyfin-ffmpeg; with another ffmpeg, HDR falls back to the CPU and is much slower.
- Settings changes: **Pause receiving tasks (暂停接收任务)** is gone, as the app takes jobs while it is open; **Max concurrency (最大并发)** is now Settings → Transcoding → **Simultaneous transcodes (同时转码)**.

#### See and sign out every login

You couldn't see where your account was signed in, and only administrators could manage command-line and transcoder access. Settings → **Devices (设备)** now lists the browsers, apps, command-line tools, transcoders and Jellyfin players signed in to your account, with system, version and last activity. Every member can rename their own devices or **Sign out (注销)** any of them, and administrators can switch to every member's devices.

- Approve a new `mclaw` or transcoder by entering its pairing code, or by opening the link it shows; the pending-requests list is gone.
- Members can pair `mclaw` with their own account, and it gets that member's permissions. Only administrators can approve transcoders.
- Changing your password signs out your other browsers, apps and Jellyfin players. Tick **Also sign out command line and transcoders (同时注销命令行和转码器)** to sign those out too.
- Existing `mclaw` and transcoder pairings keep working after the update, and `mclaw logout` now also signs the machine out on the server.

### New features

- **Subscriptions page (Silver Glass)**: A rotating hero with title logos and backdrops, a **Just arrived (刚刚入库)** row, a week-ahead schedule, and separate movie and show rows with **View all (查看全部)** in place of the type switch.
- **Rule groups chosen automatically**: Give a group an **Applies to (适用范围)** (movies or shows, regions, genres) under Settings → Subscription Rules. New subscriptions pick the matching group, otherwise the default. Existing subscriptions keep their groups.
- **Different themes on computer and phone**: Settings → Appearance now sets the theme for the kind of device you are on.
- **How each stream plays**: Activity marks each playback as direct, remux or transcode, and shows where it is being transcoded.
- **Clean up leftover boost torrents**: When you turn off ratio boost or delete a site, you can delete its boost torrents and their files. Later, use **Clean up boost torrents… (清理刷流种子…)** in Activity. Torrents still inside the site's required seeding time are deleted when it ends.

### Improvements

- **Faster**: Resuming starts sooner, saving a subscription no longer pauses for seconds, and the subscriptions and library pages load faster.
- **Less disk reading**: Opening a title's details no longer reads the whole file to extract subtitles; embedded subtitles are extracted when the player asks for them.
- **Web player**: The speed in the player shows what is actually loading. In the diagnostics panel, **Stream (取流)** is now **Bandwidth (带宽)**. Chapter marks are gone from the progress bar; chapter names still show while scrubbing.
- **Phone layout (Silver Glass)**: The bottom bar is icon-only. **More (更多)** is now the **Me (我的)** tab with your avatar, and search is at the top right. Start new conversations from Me → Recent conversations; copying a conversation ID is only in the desktop sidebar.

### Fixed

- Playing in Infuse through a remote transcoder always failed (needs the new Mac transcoder).
- Newer Jellyfin clients such as Findroid, Flow and the official Android TV app may have refused to connect because the server reported an old version.
- With Docker bridge networking, players that found the server through local discovery got an address they couldn't reach. Discovery now returns Settings → Network → **External access address (外部访问地址)**; fill in your LAN address there and map UDP port 7359.
- The scheduled task switches were misaligned in Chrome and Edge, and the rule group list was squashed on phones.

### For administrators

- Resetting a member's password signs them out of web, app and players, but not the command line. Use **Sign out everywhere (全部下线)** in Settings → Members to sign them out everywhere.
- Playback quality records are now deleted from the server after 90 days.

### Before you update

- This release migrates the database. To go back, open Settings → Update & Maintenance → **Choose a version to roll back to… (选择版本回退…)** and pick v0.27.0. It restores the automatic backup made before this update, so changes made since are lost.
- MovieClaw is now under the MovieClaw License (Apache-2.0 with non-commercial terms) instead of MIT. Personal and family use is not affected.

## 简体中文

## v0.28.0：MovieClaw 有了 iPhone App

第一个原生 iPhone App 来了：在 iPhone 上浏览片库、订阅、播放，蓝光原盘目录和 ISO 也能直接放。Mac 转码器改成状态面板，也能转蓝光原盘；新的「设备」页列出你的账号在哪些地方登录着。

> ✅ 本版无需更新 Docker 镜像，在「设置 → 更新与维护」里更新即可。
> ⚠️ **iPhone App**：需要本版服务器，旧服务器上 App 登录不了。
> ⚠️ **用 Mac 转码器的**：下载 [MovieClawTranscoder-macos-arm64.zip](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.28.0/MovieClawTranscoder-macos-arm64.zip) 替换旧版。App 改名为「MovieClaw 转码器」，请删掉旧的「MovieClaw Transcoder.app」，免得出现两个（设置和配对会沿用）。旧版仍能用，但蓝光原盘和 Infuse 的转码不会交给它，改由服务器自己转或直接播放原文件。

### 本版亮点

#### 片库变成真正的 iPhone App

以前在 iPhone 上只能开网页，大文件和原盘常常要转码或者放不了。新 App 直接从服务器播原文件，4K HDR、蓝光原盘目录、DVD、ISO 都能放，支持画中画和隔空播放。下载 [MovieClaw-iOS-unsigned.ipa](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.28.0/MovieClaw-iOS-unsigned.ipa)，用 AltStore、SideStore 或 Sideloadly 以自己的 Apple ID 安装。

- 需要 iOS 26 或更新版本。只适配 iPhone（iPad 上以兼容模式运行），界面只有中文。
- 填服务器地址、用户名和密码登录；首次打开会在局域网里找服务器。可以保存多台服务器、多个账号。
- 订阅规则、资源站点、下载器、自动入库在网页端配置，App 里会引导你去网页。
- 用免费 Apple ID 签的包 7 天后过期，AltStore、SideStore 可以自动续签。

#### Mac 转码器，打开就能用

Mac 转码器以前是菜单栏图标加设置窗口，现在是一块状态面板，看得到正在转哪部片。它开机自启，转码内核崩溃后会自动重启。蓝光原盘目录能直接转，HDR 和杜比视界在显卡上转成 SDR。安装包经过 Apple 公证，下载后双击即可打开。

- 需要 Apple 芯片的 Mac，macOS 12 或更新版本。ISO 和 DVD 暂时不能转。
- 在显卡上转 HDR 需要 Jellyfin-ffmpeg；换成其他 ffmpeg 时 HDR 改用 CPU，会慢很多。
- 设置变化：「暂停接收任务」去掉了，App 开着就接任务；「最大并发」改到「设置 → 转码 → 同时转码」。

#### 看清并注销每一处登录

以前看不到账号在哪里登录着，命令行和转码器也只有管理员能管。现在「设置 → 设备」列出登录着你账号的浏览器、App、命令行、转码器和 Jellyfin 播放器，带系统、版本和最近活跃时间。每位成员都能给自己的设备改名，或把任意一台「注销」；管理员还可以切换查看全部成员的设备。

- 新的 `mclaw` 或转码器，输入它显示的配对码、或打开它给出的链接即可批准；待批准请求列表去掉了。
- 成员可以用自己的账号给 `mclaw` 配对，它只有这个成员的权限。转码器只能由管理员批准。
- 修改密码会注销你的其他浏览器、App 和 Jellyfin 播放器；勾选「同时注销命令行和转码器」才会一并注销它们。
- 已配对的 `mclaw` 和转码器更新后照常可用，不用重新配对；`mclaw logout` 现在会同时在服务器上注销这台机器。

### 新功能

- **订阅页（银玻璃）**：顶部是带片名 Logo 和剧照的轮播大图，下面有「刚刚入库」、一周更新日程，电影和剧集分成两行、用「查看全部」进入，不再有类型切换。
- **自动选规则组**：在「设置 → 订阅规则」给规则组设「适用范围」（电影或剧集、区域、类型）。新订阅自动选中匹配的组，都不匹配时用默认组。已有订阅的规则组不变。
- **电脑和手机用不同主题**：「设置 → 外观」现在设置的是你当前这类设备的主题。
- **看得出怎么在播**：活动页标出每个播放是直连、重封装还是转码，转码时显示在哪台机器上转。
- **清理残留的刷流种子**：关闭刷流或删除站点时，可以连同数据文件删掉该站的刷流种子；事后也能在活动页用「清理刷流种子…」清。还没做满站点要求做种时长的，到期后再自动删。

### 体验改进

- **更快**：续播起播更快，保存订阅不再卡几秒，订阅页和媒体库页打开更快。
- **少读盘**：打开影片详情不再通读整个文件来抽字幕，内封字幕改为播放器需要时再抽。
- **网页播放器**：播放器里的速度改为显示实际正在加载的速度。诊断面板的「取流」改名「带宽」。进度条去掉章节刻度，拖动时仍显示章节名。
- **手机版布局（银玻璃）**：底栏只留图标。「更多」变成显示头像的「我的」页签，搜索挪到右上角。新会话从「我的 → 最近会话」开始；「复制会话 ID」只在电脑版侧栏里还有。

### 修好了

- 用 Infuse 播放、经远程转码器转码时总是失败的问题已修复（需要新版 Mac 转码器）。
- Findroid、Flow、Jellyfin 官方 Android TV 版等新版 Jellyfin 客户端，可能因服务器报的版本太旧而拒绝连接，这个问题已修复。
- Docker 桥接网络下，播放器在局域网自动发现到的地址连不上。现在自动发现会返回「设置 → 网络 → 外部访问地址」，在那里填上局域网地址，并映射 UDP 7359 端口即可。
- 定时任务开关在 Chrome、Edge 里错位，订阅规则页的规则组列表在手机上变形，这两个问题已修复。

### 管理员须知

- 重置成员密码会让他在网页、App 和播放器上下线，命令行不受影响；要全部收回，在「设置 → 成员」里用「全部下线」。
- 播放质量记录现在在服务器上保留 90 天后删除。

### 升级须知

- 本版包含数据库迁移。要回退，到「设置 → 更新与维护 → 选择版本回退…」选 v0.27.0，会恢复这次更新前的自动备份，更新之后的改动会丢失。
- 项目许可证从 MIT 改为 MovieClaw 许可证（Apache-2.0 附加非商业条件），个人和家庭自用不受影响。

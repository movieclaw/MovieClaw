## iPhone and iPad 0.12.2: The air schedule is easier to scan

On a series, the season progress bar is gone. Episodes opens with what airs today or next.

> ✅ Works with MovieClaw Server 0.28.0 or later, the same as 0.12.1. This build is the unsigned IPA at https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/tag/ios-v0.12.2. It is not on TestFlight.

### Highlights

#### The air schedule reads as two lines

Open a series that still has an air date today or later. Under **Episodes**, the progress bar and its legend are gone. The first line names what airs today or next, such as **S02E03–E04 airs**, with the expected arrival on the right. The second line is the cadence, how many episodes are left, and the finale. **Air schedule** opens the sheet: a large remaining count, a collapsed summary of what already aired with **Find this episode**, and rows grouped into **This week**, **Next week**, and **Later**. The weekday sits on its own line. The date, the dot, the episode number, and the status share one row. A gap appears only when a real break skips a cadence weekday. The same layout is on the website in Server 0.46.0. On an older server the schedule still uses the dates that server already sends.

Full changes: [ios-v0.12.1 → ios-v0.12.2](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/ios-v0.12.1...ios-v0.12.2).

## 简体中文

## iPhone 与 iPad 0.12.2：播出日历更好读

剧集详情里，本季进度条没有了。分集标题下先写今天或下次更新哪几集。

> ✅ 搭配 MovieClaw 服务器 0.28.0 或更新版本即可使用，与 0.12.1 相同。这一版是未签名 IPA：https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/tag/ios-v0.12.2。没有上传到 TestFlight。

### 本版亮点

#### 播出日历改成两行

打开一部本季还有今天或以后播出日期的剧。在「分集」下面，进度条和图例没有了。第一行写今天或下次更新的集，例如「S02E03–E04更新」，右边是预计入库时间。第二行是更新节奏、还剩几集，以及大结局。点「播出日历」打开面板：上面是还剩多少集，已播出的摘要可以收起，里面有「补这一集」；下面按「本周」「下周」「之后」分组。星期单独一行。日期、圆点、集数和状态在同一行上对齐。只有真的跳过了一个播出日，才会出现停更分隔。网页要服务器 0.46.0 才是同一套版式。更早的服务器上，日历仍用那台服务器已经给出的播出日期。

完整改动：[ios-v0.12.1 → ios-v0.12.2](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/ios-v0.12.1...ios-v0.12.2)。

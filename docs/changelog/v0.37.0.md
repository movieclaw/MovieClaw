## v0.37.0: Find your next film without leaving the library

Library detail pages now suggest similar titles and lay out a film's whole series. Notifications for your phone and your messaging apps are now chosen in one place, with five new kinds.

> ⚠️ **Update the Docker image**: Stop the container and back up the data directory, then run `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`. In-app update cannot install this version; it shows that a new Docker image is required.

### Highlights

#### Pick the next film from the page you're on

When a film ended, finding something like it meant searching again, and its series was only a link. Under the cast, library detail pages now show **Similar (相似推荐)**, and for a film in a series, the whole series in release order with how many you have. This works on the web, iPhone, Mac, Android and Android TV, and appears without any setup.

- Suggestions come only from libraries of the same type that you can see. Animation, documentaries and reality shows are not mixed with other titles.
- The series row needs TMDB collection data. Series set only by local NFO files don't show it.
- Missing films in a series are dimmed. On the web, iPhone and Android you can open them to subscribe.
- Android also adds a **Related links (相关链接)** row for TMDB, IMDb and Douban.
- Mac and Android TV show the cast as portrait photo cards.

#### Phone and chat notifications, set in one place

Messaging apps such as Telegram and WeChat used to have two server-wide switches of their own, separate from phone notifications. Now a linked messaging account is one more channel for its owner and follows the same choices as their phone. New notifications cover a subscription that has every episode, a stalled download, a season that may have more episodes, a problem that has cleared, and an AI reply that has finished. Start at Settings → Account → **Notifications (通知)**.

- The test notification on that page now also goes to your messaging accounts.
- **AI reply finished (AI 回复完成)** is for administrators, goes to phones only, and covers questions asked in the app or on the web. Sideloaded iPhone builds can't receive push; Android needs realtime notifications turned on.
- Administrators are also told when a messaging account's sign-in expires and it needs to be linked again.
- Notifications from plugins appear in the same list, grouped by plugin.

### New features

- **Android TV episode controls**: During playback, press Up for **Next episode (下一集)** and **Episodes (选集)**.
- **AI chat on iPhone** (administrators): Conversations read as chat bubbles; long-press a bubble to copy it or rewrite your question. The Me (我的) tab has a one-line AI input above the tab bar.
- **Network-drive files on local mounts**: strm files that point to a path on the server, such as a CloudDrive2 or rclone mount, now play like local files. The path must be reachable inside the container at the same location.
- **Fewer relays for network drives**: When a strm link goes through a 302 service on your network, players are sent straight to the final address if they can reach it.

### Improvements

- **Up next**: If end credits aren't recognized, an episode now plays to the end, then counts down to the next one. After three episodes in a row with no input, it asks **Still watching? (还在看吗？)** (web, iPhone, Mac).
- **Quieter Android TV home**: Preview autoplay is now off by default. Turn it on from your account page in the sidebar with **Auto-play previews (自动播放预告)**.
- **Intel GPUs**: Hardware transcoding now uses low-power encoding when the GPU supports it. This may stop crashes seen on some Intel GPUs, such as virtualized ones.
- **Gallery view**: **Gallery view (图床浏览)** is now remembered per library. On a collection page it moved from the toolbar into the ⋯ menu.
- Plus 3 smaller improvements: intro and credit detection uses less CPU, Douban links on iPhone open the Douban app when it's installed, and linked Emby or Jellyfin servers are also refreshed after scans and manual imports.

### Fixed

- In the browser, if the server's transcode failed to start, the player kept retrying for minutes. It now drops to a lower quality right away.
- Browsing a few pages no longer fills Task Center with queued network-drive prefetch jobs. The bundled prefetch plugin is updated to 1.0.1 and clears jobs left over by the old version.
- strm files saved with a UTF-8 BOM are no longer rejected, and files saved as GB18030 are read correctly.
- Android TV: Back with the sidebar open quit the app; it now closes the sidebar.
- Android TV: Focus no longer gets stuck with only Left working after you close the sidebar or the player.
- Android TV: The detail page no longer takes seconds to slide between the artwork and the episode rows.
- Plus 2 Android TV display fixes: episode numbers shown twice under cards, and uneven Chinese text weights.

### For administrators

- Existing messaging accounts now belong to the administrator and send what the administrator chose in **Notifications**. Earlier messaging switches are not carried over.
- Changes on messaging apps: **Download started (开始下载)** and **Upgrade finished (洗版完成)** are now off by default. **Imported (入库完成)** comes back on if you had turned it off there.
- Messaging accounts now get only subscriptions the administrator started or follows. To get all new arrivals, turn on **New in your libraries (媒体库有新片)**.
- Group bots receive the same as the person who linked them, including new-device sign-ins with the device name and IP address.
- MovieClaw-format webhooks send a new message layout, with renamed fields and the `X-MovieClaw-Event-Id` header. Update your receivers. Jellyfin-format webhooks keep working with your template.
- Webhooks can now subscribe to 35 system events, up from 10, plus events that plugins declare. Failed deliveries are retried 5 times over about 40 minutes and are not lost on restart; progress and detail-view events are not retried.
- Plugin approval now lists the notifications a plugin sends and the viewing activity it reads. Notifying members needs **Allow it to notify household members (允许它给家里的成员发通知)**. Plugins built for earlier versions that listen to events may need updating.
- With host networking, if port 3001 is already used, set `MOVIECLAW_NEXT_PORT` to another port; port 8000 must still be free.

### Before you update

- This release migrates the database, and a Docker image update makes no automatic backup. To return to v0.36.0, stop the container, change the image tag to `movieclaw/movieclaw:v0.36.0`, restore the backup you made, then start it.
- Similar titles and the series row need the new apps. Older apps keep working without them. Android 0.3.1: https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.37.0/MovieClaw-Android-arm64.apk · Android TV 0.2.2: https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.37.0/MovieClaw-AndroidTV.apk
- The iPhone and Mac apps stay at 0.7.0 but are rebuilt for this release: https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.37.0/MovieClaw-iOS-unsigned.ipa · https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.37.0/MovieClaw-macos-arm64.zip. No Mac transcoder update is required.
- On Android 0.3.0, some notifications may land in a different notification category until you update to 0.3.1.

Thanks to @WadeChenn for the Android TV improvements. Full changes: [v0.36.0 → v0.37.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.36.0...v0.37.0).

## 简体中文

## v0.37.0：看完一部，在片库里接着找下一部

媒体库详情页现在会推荐相似作品，并列出这部电影所在的整个系列。手机和 IM 的通知改在同一个地方选，还新增了五种通知。

> ⚠️ **需要更新 Docker 镜像**：先停止容器并备份数据目录，再执行 `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`。应用内更新装不了这一版，会提示需要拉取新的 Docker 镜像。

### 本版亮点

#### 在当前页面就能挑下一部

以前看完一部，想找类似的片要重新搜索，所属系列也只是一个链接。现在媒体库详情页在演职员下面列出「相似推荐」；系列电影还会按上映顺序列出整个系列，并标出你已有几部。网页、iPhone、Mac、Android、Android TV 都有，升级后自动出现，不用设置。

- 只从你能看到的同类型媒体库里推荐。动画、纪录片、真人秀不会和其他片混在一起推荐。
- 系列一行需要 TMDB 的系列资料；只靠本地 NFO 定的系列不显示这一行。
- 系列里库中没有的片显示为压暗。在网页、iPhone 和 Android 上可以点开去订阅。
- Android 还新增「相关链接」一行，可跳到 TMDB、IMDb 和豆瓣。
- Mac 和 Android TV 的演职员改为竖版照片卡片。

#### 手机和 IM 的通知，在一个地方选

以前 Telegram、微信这类 IM 有自己的两个全服务器开关，和手机通知是两套。现在绑定的 IM 账号只是它主人的又一个通知渠道，收什么和他的手机一样。新增的通知有：订阅收齐了、下载卡住了、可能还有更多集、问题已恢复，以及 AI 回复完成。从「设置 → 账号 → 通知」开始。

- 这一页的测试通知现在也会发到你的 IM。
- 「AI 回复完成」只发给管理员，只推到手机、不发 IM，覆盖在 App 或网页里提的问题。侧载的 iPhone 收不到推送；Android 需要打开实时通知。
- IM 账号的登录凭据失效、需要重新绑定时，管理员也会收到提醒。
- 插件发的通知也在同一个列表里，按插件分组。

### 新功能

- **Android TV 播放选集**：播放时按上键，可以选「下一集」或「选集」。
- **iPhone 上的 AI 对话**（管理员）：对话改为聊天气泡，长按气泡可以复制或改写提问。「我的」页的标签栏上方多了一行 AI 输入框。
- **本地挂载的网盘文件**：内容是服务器路径的 strm（如 CloudDrive2、rclone 挂载）现在能像本地文件一样播放。这个路径必须在容器里也能按同样的位置访问到。
- **网盘少走中转**：strm 链接经过局域网里的 302 服务时，如果播放器能直接访问最终地址，就直接跳过去。

### 体验改进

- **自动播下一集**：没认出片尾时，这一集会放到最后，再倒计时接下一集。连续自动播了 3 集都没有操作时，会问「还在看吗？」（网页、iPhone、Mac）。
- **Android TV 首页更安静**：预告自动播放改为默认关闭。可以在侧栏的账号页打开「自动播放预告」。
- **Intel 核显**：显卡支持时，硬件转码改用低功耗编码。部分 Intel 核显（如虚拟化的核显）转码时出现的崩溃可能因此消失。
- **图床浏览**：「图床浏览」改为每个媒体库分别记住。合集页上的切换按钮从顶栏挪进了 ⋯ 菜单。
- 以及 3 处细节改进：识别片头片尾更省 CPU；iPhone 上的豆瓣链接在装了豆瓣 App 时直接打开 App；扫描和手动整理后也会通知已关联的 Emby 或 Jellyfin 刷新。

### 修好了

- 网页播放时，服务器转码起不来，播放器会反复重试好几分钟。现在会马上降一档画质继续播。
- 浏览几页就把任务中心塞满网盘预探任务的问题已修复。内置预探插件更新到 1.0.1，会清掉旧版本留下的任务。
- 带 UTF-8 BOM 的 strm 不会再被拒绝，GB18030 编码的也能正确读取。
- Android TV：打开侧栏时按返回会直接退出 App，现在只会关闭侧栏。
- Android TV：关闭侧栏或播放器后，焦点卡住、只剩左键能用的问题已修复。
- Android TV：详情页在背景图和剧集行之间滑动要好几秒，现在不会了。
- 以及 2 处 Android TV 显示问题：剧集卡片下集数显示两遍、中文字重不一致。

### 管理员须知

- 已有的 IM 账号现在都归管理员，推送内容按管理员在「通知」里的选择。原来的 IM 开关不会迁移过来。
- IM 收到的内容有变化：「开始下载」「洗版完成」现在默认关闭；原来在 IM 里关掉了「入库完成」的，升级后会重新收到。
- IM 现在只推管理员发起或关注的订阅。想收到所有新入库的片，打开「媒体库有新片」。
- 群机器人收到的和绑定人一样，包括新设备登录（带设备名和 IP 地址）。
- MovieClaw 格式的 Webhook 改用新的报文结构：字段改了名，请求头改为 `X-MovieClaw-Event-Id`。请同步更新接收端。Jellyfin 格式的 Webhook 照常使用你的模板。
- Webhook 可订阅的系统事件从 10 个增加到 35 个，插件声明的事件也能订阅。投递失败会在约 40 分钟内重试 5 次，重启后不会丢；播放进度和打开详情这两个事件不重试。
- 安装插件时的审批页会列出它发哪些通知、读取哪些观看动态。要让它给成员发通知，需要勾选「允许它给家里的成员发通知」。为旧版本写的、监听事件的插件可能需要更新。
- 使用 host 网络时，如果 3001 端口已被占用，把 `MOVIECLAW_NEXT_PORT` 设成别的端口；8000 端口仍须空闲。

### 升级须知

- 本版包含数据库迁移，更新 Docker 镜像不会自动备份。要回到 v0.36.0：停止容器，把镜像标签改回 `movieclaw/movieclaw:v0.36.0`，恢复你做的备份后再启动。
- 相似推荐和系列一行需要新版 App；旧版 App 照常能用，只是没有这两行。Android 0.3.1：https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.37.0/MovieClaw-Android-arm64.apk · Android TV 0.2.2：https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.37.0/MovieClaw-AndroidTV.apk
- iPhone 和 Mac 版仍是 0.7.0，但为这一版重新构建：https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.37.0/MovieClaw-iOS-unsigned.ipa · https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.37.0/MovieClaw-macos-arm64.zip 。本版无需更新 Mac 转码器。
- 还在用 Android 0.3.0 的，部分通知可能会落到另一个通知类别里，更新到 0.3.1 即可。

## v0.37.0: Find your next film, get the notifications you choose

Related titles now appear in your library, notifications follow each person's preferences, and Android TV gains easier episode controls. Windows adds discovery, subscriptions and AI subtitles.

> ⚠️ **Update the Docker image**: Pull `movieclaw/movieclaw:latest` and recreate the container. This release changes the container startup requirements; the in-app updater will ask for a new image.

Android updates to **0.3.1**, Android TV to **0.2.2**, and Windows to **1.0.4**. Apple version numbers remain unchanged; changed Apple apps are rebuilt through the existing release pipeline.

### Highlights

#### Pick another title without leaving the library

After finishing a film, you previously had to search for something similar or the rest of its series. Library detail pages now show **Similar titles** and **Series** separately, with recommendations drawn from titles you can access. Open a movie or series in the web, Android or Android TV app; Android also adds external detail links.

#### Choose what reaches you, on your phone and in chat

Messaging notifications now follow each person's choices alongside phone notifications. You can receive subscription completion, stalled-download and new-episode notices, resolved alerts, and a notification when the AI assistant finishes replying. Start at **Settings → Account → Notifications**; messaging delivery uses your linked account.

#### Windows can do more than play your library

The Windows client adds audio/subtitle selection before playback, AI subtitle preview and generation for administrators, and optional Discovery and My Subscriptions entries in **Settings → Navigation**. An optional watch-presence plugin lets members share viewing activity with friends; viewing and sharing are off by default.

### More features and improvements

- **Android TV episode controls**: Pick an episode or move to the next one from the player.
- **Quieter TV home**: Preview autoplay defaults off and can be enabled in settings.
- **Faster network-drive preparation**: Watching an episode prioritizes upcoming episodes within the existing prefetch job; the bundled prefetch plugin updates to **1.0.1**.
- **More reliable playback recovery**: A failed local transcode can fall back before the first picture appears.
- **Clearer endings**: Recognized end credits trigger an early next-episode reminder; otherwise playback continues to the end.

### Fixed

- Android TV's Back button closes the sidebar, and focus returns to the page after it closes.
- Android TV no longer repeats episode numbers on cards, and Chinese text uses consistent fonts and weights.
- Local-path strm files can play, and streams behind LAN redirects are served through the server when needed.
- Library browsing preferences stay scoped to each library.

### Before you update

- This release migrates the database. To return to v0.36.0 or earlier, restore the automatic pre-update backup.
- Windows packages pass hosted CI browser, native-player and installation checks. They may be unsigned and require confirmation; physical GPU/HDR, audio passthrough and multi-monitor acceptance remain incomplete.

## v0.37.0：接着找片，按你想要的方式收通知

媒体库详情页现在能找相似作品与同系列电影；通知按每个人的偏好推送；Android TV 选集更方便。Windows 新增发现、订阅和 AI 字幕。

> ⚠️ **需要更新 Docker 镜像**：请拉取 `movieclaw/movieclaw:latest` 并重建容器。本版调整了容器启动要求，应用内更新会提示先更新镜像。

Android 更新到 **0.3.1**，Android TV 更新到 **0.2.2**，Windows 更新到 **1.0.4**。苹果产品版本号保持不变，有改动的苹果客户端仍按现有发布流程重新构建。

### 本版亮点

#### 看完一部，在片库里继续找下一部

以前想找风格相近的片或同系列续作，要重新搜索。现在详情页分别列出「相似推荐」与「作品系列」，只推荐你有权限访问的片。网页、Android 和 Android TV 打开电影或剧集详情即可查看；Android 还增加了外部详情链接。

#### 手机和聊天软件，都按你的偏好推送

聊天软件通知现在和手机通知一样，遵循每个人自己的选择。订阅完成、下载卡住、新集上线、告警解除，以及 AI 助手回复完成都可以收到提醒。从「设置 → 账号 → 通知」开始选择；聊天软件通过你绑定的账号接收。

#### Windows 不只用来播片

Windows 客户端支持播放前选择音轨和字幕，管理员可预览、生成 AI 字幕；在「设置 → 导航」开启「发现」与「我的订阅」。可选的观看动态插件让成员向好友分享观影动态，查看和分享默认都关闭。

### 其他新功能与改进

- **Android TV 播放选集**：播放器内可选集，也能直接切下一集。
- **电视首页更安静**：预告自动播放默认关闭，可在设置中开启。
- **网盘准备更及时**：开始看片后在已有预探任务中优先准备后续集；内置预探插件更新到 **1.0.1**。
- **起播失败能继续尝试**：本地转码在出画前失败时自动尝试降档。
- **片尾提示更自然**：识别到片尾时提前提示下一集，否则播完再接续。

### 修好了

- Android TV 按返回会关闭侧栏，关闭后焦点回到原页面。
- Android TV 剧集卡片不再重复集数，中文字体与字重更一致。
- 本地路径的 strm 能正常播放，必要时通过服务器中转内网跳转后的码流。
- 媒体库浏览偏好分别保存，各片库互不影响。

### 升级须知

- 本版升级了数据库。如需回退到 v0.36.0 或更早版本，请用更新前自动生成的备份恢复。
- Windows 安装包通过托管 CI 的浏览器、原生播放与安装验证；可能未签名，安装时需确认。实体显卡/HDR、音频直通与多显示器验收尚未完成。

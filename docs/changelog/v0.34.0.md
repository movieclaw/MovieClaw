## v0.34.0: MovieClaw comes to Android TV

A native Android TV app joins the family, with better disc-image playback and watch statistics that reflect real viewing.

✅ No Docker image update is required. Administrators can update from **Settings → Update & Maintenance**.
Update the Apple apps to **0.7.0** and Android to **0.1.1**. Android TV starts at **0.1.0**.

### Highlights

#### Browse and watch from your Android TV remote

Connect your TV to MovieClaw, choose who's watching, browse libraries and search with the remote. Continue watching, switch audio and subtitles, skip intros and publish upcoming episodes to the system's Play Next row. Download the Android TV APK from this Release; this first version is for early testing, and playback capabilities vary by TV or box.

#### More disc images can play on clients that need server processing

Some Blu-ray and DVD ISO files could not start, or showed the wrong duration. The server now reads their main title, including titles split across the image, so it can prepare playback for clients that need it. Existing disc-image durations are corrected automatically after upgrading.

#### Watch statistics count what you actually watched

The Activity page now counts viewings and distributes watch time across the hours you watched. Playback quality statistics exclude testing sessions, and completion reflects the viewing session. Open **Activity** after updating; no configuration is needed.

### More features and improvements

- **Clearer iPhone feedback**: App messages appear near the bottom and remain visible above sheets and the keyboard.
- **Recognizable devices**: Device and playback lists show Android models and avoid repeated client names.
- **Less unnecessary TV transcoding**: Compatible Dolby Vision content can use the TV's supported playback capabilities.
- **Clearer busy-transcoder messages**: Playback distinguishes a busy transcoder from missing hardware.

### Fixed

- Android HTTPS playback no longer crashes on the affected native-player path.
- Two devices starting the same title for the first time no longer cause a server error.
- Reopening a title no longer gets blocked by your own previous transcoding session.

### Before you update

- This release migrates the database. To return to v0.33.0 or earlier, restore the automatic pre-update backup.
- Android TV is a separate app from the Android phone/tablet client and requires Android 6.0 or later.
- No Mac transcoder update is required for this release.

Thanks to @WadeChenn for the Android HTTPS playback fix.

Full changes: [v0.33.0 → v0.34.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.33.0...v0.34.0).

## 简体中文

## v0.34.0：Android 电视也能用 MovieClaw 看片

这一版带来 Android TV 原生 App，让更多光盘镜像能正常播放，观看统计也更贴近你实际看的内容。

✅ 本版无需更新 Docker 镜像，管理员在「设置 → 更新与维护」一键更新即可。
Apple App 请更新到 **0.7.0**，Android 更新到 **0.1.1**；Android TV 首版为 **0.1.0**。

### 本版亮点

#### 拿起 Android 电视遥控器就能逛片库、看片

连接 MovieClaw 服务器，选择「谁在看」，用遥控器浏览片库和搜索影片。可以继续观看、切换音轨与字幕、跳过片头，也能把待看的剧集放到系统的「接下来播放」栏。
在本版 Release 附件中下载 Android TV APK。首版面向早期测试，不同电视和盒子的播放能力有所差异。

#### 需要服务器处理的客户端，也能播放更多光盘镜像

以前部分蓝光、DVD ISO 镜像无法起播，或显示的总时长不对。服务器现在能读取主片，包括分散存放的主片片段，为需要服务器处理的客户端准备播放内容。
升级后会自动修正已有光盘镜像的时长，不用手动重建片库。

#### 观看统计按实际看的内容来算

「动态」页现在按观看次数统计，把跨小时观看的时长分配到实际经过的小时。播放质量统计排除测试会话，是否看完也按这次观看判断。
升级后打开「动态」即可查看，不用额外设置。

### 其他新功能与改进

- **iPhone 提示更清楚**：提示靠近屏幕底部，在弹层和键盘上方也能看见。
- **设备更好认**：设备和播放列表显示 Android 机型，不再重复客户端名称。
- **电视少做无谓转码**：兼容的杜比视界片源按电视支持的播放能力处理。
- **转码器忙时说清楚**：区分转码器暂时忙碌和缺少硬件的情况。

### 修好了

- 修复 Android 原生播放器在受影响的 HTTPS 播放路径上崩溃的问题。
- 两台设备首次同时播放同一影片时，不再出现服务器错误。
- 重新打开影片时，不再被自己上一次的转码会话挡住。

### 升级须知

- 本版升级了数据库。如需回退到 v0.33.0 或更早版本，请用更新前自动生成的备份恢复。
- Android TV 与手机、平板版是独立 App，要求 Android 6.0 或更高版本。
- 本版无需更新 Mac 转码器。

感谢 @WadeChenn 修复 Android HTTPS 播放问题。

完整改动见 [v0.33.0 → v0.34.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.33.0...v0.34.0)。

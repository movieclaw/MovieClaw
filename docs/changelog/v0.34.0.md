## v0.34.0: MovieClaw on your Android TV

MovieClaw now has an Android TV app. ISO disc images also play in the web browser, and watch statistics count what people actually watched.

> ✅ No Docker image update is required. Update from Settings → Update & Maintenance.

### Highlights

#### Watch from the couch with your Android TV remote

On an Android TV or box you could only use a Jellyfin player. The new Android TV app shows your home rows, libraries and collections, searches with the remote, and plays with subtitle, audio and quality panels, skip intro and an up-next card. Download https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.34.0/MovieClaw-AndroidTV.apk, install it on the TV, pick your server, then scan the QR code it shows with your phone and approve. This first version is for early testing, and playback can differ between TVs and boxes.

- Needs Android 6.0 or later on an ARM TV or box, and MovieClaw server v0.34.0 or later. Older servers refuse its sign-in.
- Approve with your phone's camera, the scan button at the top right of Me (我的) in the iPhone app, or by entering the pairing code at the address the TV shows. The TV signs in as whoever approves and appears in their Settings → Devices (设备). A user name and password also work.
- Keep several accounts, even on different servers, and switch under **Who's watching? (谁在看？)**.
- It plays the original file when the TV can, otherwise the server transcodes. Dolby Vision the TV supports isn't tone-mapped, and ASS subtitles and DTS or TrueHD audio play in the app. The TV switches to the video's frame rate; on Android 12 and later this follows the system's frame-rate matching setting.
- Resting on the large artwork plays a short clip with sound, with no switch to turn it off. Episodes to continue go to the system's Play Next row, which some TVs lack.
- It is made for watching: no Discover, subscriptions, Reels, settings, downloads or push notifications. The interface is in Chinese.

#### ISO disc images play in the browser too

Blu-ray and DVD ISO images used to play only in players that read them directly, such as the iPhone and Android apps or Infuse; the web player refused them, and so did the apps once you lowered the quality. Now the server reads the film inside the image and streams it, so ISO titles play in the web browser, on Android TV and in the apps at a lower quality, including discs whose film is split into many pieces. It works after the update, with no setup.

- An image the server can't read is still refused, with a message suggesting a player that opens ISO files itself.

#### Watch statistics that match real viewing

Every open used to count as a play, so a title opened for a few seconds could top **Most popular (本期最受欢迎)**, and scrubbing added to watch time. Now a play needs at least a minute of watching or reaching the end, picking up where you stopped within 3 hours (even on another device) counts as the same viewing, and watch time can't exceed the time that actually passed. Administrators find it under Activity → **Watch Statistics (观看统计)**, with no setup.

- Finished means the viewing reached the last 10% and at least a third of it was actually watched. Rewatching to the end now counts; jumping straight to the end doesn't.
- The heatmap and daily chart spread watch time across the hours it spans, so a late movie no longer lands on one hour or the wrong day.
- **By playback method (按播放方式)** leaves out plays under a minute, and its note now correctly says it covers the web and the apps.
- The change from the previous period appears only when history covers that whole period.
- Share guests' watch time counts, but they are not counted as active members.
- Play counts for past periods follow the new rules, so they can drop. Watch time recorded before this version is not recalculated.

### More features and improvements

- **Clearer transcoder messages**: When an HDR title needs the Mac transcoder and it is online but full, the message says it's busy and asks you to try again, instead of saying no hardware was found.
- **iPhone messages**: Pop-up messages now sit at the bottom, above sheets and the keyboard. Swipe down or tap to dismiss.
- **Device names in Activity**: New plays from Android phones and TVs show the device model, and the client name is no longer repeated.

### Fixed

- Some ISO episodes and films were marked watched as soon as they started, kept no resume point and showed a wrong duration. Stored durations are corrected in the background after the update. Titles already marked watched stay marked; use **Mark as unwatched (标记为未看)** on the title page.
- The Android app could crash on some phones when playing certain titles from a server reached over HTTPS. Thanks to @WadeChenn.
- After an app was force-closed during transcoded playback, starting another title on the same phone or TV could be refused for up to 3 minutes when the server had no other free transcoding slot.
- Reopening an HDR title, or switching its subtitles or audio, could fail with "no hardware found" when only the Mac transcoder could tone-map it.
- Plus 1 smaller fix.

### For administrators

- At every startup, the server reads the structure of each ISO image in the background, a few small reads per image. Startup doesn't wait for it.
- After the update, the server reads the header of each Dolby Vision file once in the background.
- After each playback, the Android TV app sends diagnostics to your own server: TV model, decoders, display modes and error logs. Nothing goes to the developers.

### Before you update

- This release migrates the database. Updating from Settings → Update & Maintenance first makes an automatic backup. To return to v0.33.0, go to Settings → Update & Maintenance → **Choose a version to roll back to… (选择版本回退…)**; it restores that backup, so anything recorded after the update is lost.
- If you update by pulling the Docker image instead, no backup is made: stop the container and back up the data directory first. To return, stop the container, change the image tag to `movieclaw/movieclaw:v0.33.0`, restore that backup, then start it.
- The iPhone changes need iPhone app 0.7.0: https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.34.0/MovieClaw-iOS-unsigned.ipa, installed over the old app the same way. The Android fix needs Android 0.1.1: https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.34.0/MovieClaw-Android-arm64.apk. Older apps keep working with this server.
- The Android TV app is separate from the Android phone app; both can be installed on the same device.

Thanks to @WadeChenn. Full changes: [v0.33.0 → v0.34.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.33.0...v0.34.0).

## 简体中文

## v0.34.0：Android 电视也能用 MovieClaw 看片

MovieClaw 有了 Android TV App。ISO 光盘镜像在网页里也能放了，观看统计也改成按实际看的内容来算。

> ✅ 本版无需更新 Docker 镜像，在「设置 → 更新与维护」更新即可。

### 本版亮点

#### 拿起 Android 电视遥控器就能看片

以前在 Android 电视或盒子上只能用 Jellyfin 播放器。新的 Android TV App 能看首页各行、媒体库和合集，用遥控器搜索，播放时有字幕、音轨、画质面板，能跳过片头，还有下一集卡片。下载 https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.34.0/MovieClaw-AndroidTV.apk 装到电视上，选好服务器，再用手机扫电视上的二维码并批准即可。首版面向早期测试，不同电视和盒子的播放表现可能不同。

- 需要 Android 6.0 或更高、ARM 芯片的电视或盒子，以及 MovieClaw 服务器 v0.34.0 或更新版本。更旧的服务器会拒绝它登录。
- 可以用手机相机或 iPhone App「我的」页右上角的扫码批准，也可以在浏览器打开电视上显示的地址输入配对码。谁批准，电视就登录成谁，并出现在这个人的「设置 → 设备」里。也可以用账号密码登录。
- 可以保存多个账号（可跨服务器），在「谁在看？」里切换。
- 电视能放的直接放原文件，放不了的由服务器转码。电视支持的杜比视界不做色调映射；ASS 字幕和 DTS、TrueHD 音轨由 App 自己处理。画面会切到影片的帧率；Android 12 及以上按系统的「匹配内容帧率」设置来。
- 在大图上停一会儿会带声音播放一小段片段，没有关闭开关。待继续的剧集会放到系统的「接下来播放」行，部分电视没有这一行。
- 它专为看片：没有发现、订阅、片段、设置、下载和推送通知。界面只有中文。

#### ISO 光盘镜像在网页里也能放

以前蓝光、DVD 的 ISO 镜像只能用能直接读镜像的播放器放，比如 iPhone、Android App 或 Infuse；网页播放器会提示放不了，App 调低画质后也一样。现在服务器能读出镜像里的正片并串流出去，网页、Android TV 和调低画质的 App 都能放 ISO，包括正片在镜像里分成很多段的光盘。升级后自动生效，不用设置。

- 服务器读不出的镜像仍会拒绝播放，并建议改用能直接打开 ISO 的播放器。

#### 观看统计按实际看的来算

以前每打开一次都算一次播放，点开几秒的片子也能冲上「本期最受欢迎」，来回拖动还会算进观看时长。现在至少看满 1 分钟或看到结尾才算一次播放，3 小时内从停下的地方接着看（换了设备也一样）仍算同一次，观看时长也不会超过实际经过的时间。管理员在「活动 → 观看统计」查看，不用设置。

- 「看完」指这次观看到了最后 10%，且其中至少三分之一是真的在看。重看到结尾现在也算；直接拖到结尾不算。
- 热力图和每日走势把观看时长分摊到实际经过的各个小时，深夜看的电影不会全堆在一个小时或算到错的那天。
- 「按播放方式」排除不满 1 分钟的播放，说明文字也改正为统计网页和各 App。
- 只有记录覆盖了整个上一周期，才显示与上期的对比。
- 分享访客的时长照算，但不计入活跃成员。
- 过去各周期的播放次数也按新口径算，可能变少。本版之前记下的观看时长不会重新计算。

### 其他新功能与改进

- **转码器忙时说清楚**：HDR 片要靠 Mac 转码器、而它在线但名额已满时，会提示转码器正忙、请稍后再试，不再说没有检测到硬件。
- **iPhone 提示**：弹出的提示改到屏幕底部，在弹层和键盘上方也能看到。下滑或点按即可收起。
- **「活动」里的设备名**：Android 手机和电视的新播放记录显示机型，也不会再重复客户端名。

### 修好了

- 部分 ISO 剧集和电影一开播就被标成已看、不留续播点、片长显示不对的问题已修复。升级后会在后台自动校正已有镜像的片长。已经被误标已看的不会自动恢复，可以在影片页点「标记为未看」。
- 部分手机上，Android App 通过 HTTPS 访问服务器播放某些片子时可能闪退的问题已修复。感谢 @WadeChenn。
- App 在转码播放时被强行关掉后，服务器没有其他空闲转码名额时，同一台手机或电视最多 3 分钟内开不了新片的问题已修复。
- 只有 Mac 转码器能做色调映射时，重新打开 HDR 片或切换字幕、音轨会报「未检测到硬件」的问题已修复。
- 以及 1 处细节修复。

### 管理员须知

- 每次启动后，服务器会在后台把每个 ISO 镜像的盘内结构读一遍，每个镜像只读几小块，不耽误启动。
- 升级后，服务器会在后台把每个杜比视界文件的文件头读一遍，只读一次。
- 每次播放结束，Android TV App 会把诊断信息发给你自己的服务器：电视型号、解码器、显示模式和出错日志。不会发给开发者。

### 升级须知

- 本版包含数据库迁移。在「设置 → 更新与维护」更新时会先自动备份。要回到 v0.33.0，到「设置 → 更新与维护 → 选择版本回退…」，它会恢复这份备份，更新之后记录的内容会丢失。
- 如果是拉取 Docker 镜像更新，不会自动备份：请先停止容器并备份数据目录。回退时停止容器，把镜像标签改回 `movieclaw/movieclaw:v0.33.0`，恢复这份备份后再启动。
- iPhone 上的改动需要 iPhone App 0.7.0：https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.34.0/MovieClaw-iOS-unsigned.ipa ，按原来的方式覆盖安装。Android 的修复需要 Android 0.1.1：https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.34.0/MovieClaw-Android-arm64.apk 。旧版 App 连这一版服务器照常能用。
- Android TV App 与 Android 手机版是两个独立的 App，同一台设备上可以都装。

## v0.26.0: Give your library a new look

A Netflix-style theme changes the whole interface on desktop and mobile. Subscriptions also save bandwidth by downloading only the episodes missing from a season pack.

> ✅ No Docker image update is required. Update from **Settings → Update & Maintenance**.

### Highlights

#### Switch to a Netflix-style interface

MovieClaw previously offered one Silver Glass interface with background and glass adjustments. The new theme changes the shell: a top navigation bar on desktop, Discover, Library, Subscriptions and Me tabs on mobile, and a large library hero with playback that resumes your unfinished episode. Choose the theme in Settings → Appearance. It follows your account, so each family member can choose their own. Netflix-style landscape cards and hover previews are still in development; list rows use portrait posters in this release.

#### Download just the episodes you are missing

If only one or two episodes are missing and the available resource is a season pack, subscriptions now skip the other files instead of downloading the whole season again. When episode identities are uncertain, the whole pack is downloaded to avoid missing content. This takes effect automatically after updating.

### More features and improvements

- **Pinned collections appear in Infuse**: Choose Show on Home from a collection's More Actions menu.
- **Sharper detail backdrops**: Large displays avoid soft backgrounds and the initial blurry-to-sharp transition.
- **Posters fade in after loading** instead of appearing abruptly.

### Fixed

- Corrected startup failures on Windows deployments.
- Audio, subtitle and settings menus no longer extend beyond the right edge on mobile.
- Navigation arrows appear correctly on horizontally scrolling rows, including discovery.
- Swiping cast and trailer rows on mobile is less likely to open another page accidentally.

### Before you update

- The collection action for setting a library's favorites scope is removed. Set Favorites Scope when editing the library instead.

Thanks to @duchanghexuhe for their contributions.

## 简体中文

## v0.26.0：给片库换一身新衣服

这一版多了一套 Netflix 主题，电脑和手机上的整个界面都能换成影院 App 的样子。
订阅追剧也更省了：只缺一两集时，不会再把整季包重新下一遍。

> ✅ 本版无需更新 Docker 镜像，在「设置 → 更新与维护」一键更新即可。

### 本版亮点

#### 想换个风格？一键换成 Netflix 的样子

以前 movieclaw 只有银玻璃一种界面，能调的只是背景图和玻璃质感。现在多了 Netflix
主题，换的是整个外壳：电脑上是顶部导航栏，手机上是底部「发现、媒体库、订阅、我的」
四个标签；媒体库页顶是一张全屏大图，没看完的那一集点「播放」就能接着看。
在「设置 → 外观」的「主题」里点一下即可切换，跟着账号走，家里每个人可以各选各的。
Netflix 式的横版卡片和悬停预览还在做，这一版的片单行仍是竖版海报。

#### 追剧只缺一两集，不用再整季重下

订阅的剧只缺一两集、搜到的却只有整季包时，以前会把整季重新下一遍，既费流量，
也容易让重复的版本混进片库。现在只下你缺的那几集，包里其余文件自动跳过；
拿不准哪个文件是哪一集时，宁可整包下完也不漏下。升级后自动生效，不用设置。

### 其他新功能与改进

- **钉在首页的合集，Infuse 里也能直接找到**：合集页「更多操作 → 显示在首页」即可。
- **详情页背景更清晰**：大屏上不再发虚，也不会先糊一下再变清楚。
- **海报加载完成后淡入**，不再突然跳出来。

### 修好了

- 在 Windows 上部署时应用启动不起来，现在正常了。
- 手机上看片时，音轨、字幕、设置菜单会超出屏幕右边，现在不会了。
- 发现页等横向滚动的片单，左右翻页箭头有时一直不出现，现在正常了。
- 手机上左右滑动演员、预告片时，容易误点进别的页面，现在不会了。

### 升级须知

- 合集页「更多操作」里的「设为本库的收藏范围」已下线，请在编辑媒体库时的「收藏范围」里设置。

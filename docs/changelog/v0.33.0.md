## v0.33.0: Easier subscriptions, and another way to watch

Smart torrent selection and a native Android app arrive. Expand download notifications by series, with easier-to-find settings on the web and iPhone.

✅ No Docker image update is required. Administrators can update from **Settings → Update & Maintenance**.
Update the Apple app to **0.6.0** as well. Mac transcoder users should download its update from this Release.

### Highlights

#### Set your preferences and let the server choose torrents

Choose Smart Selection when subscribing on the web, then set movie and series quality preferences, minimum requirements and how long to wait. Subscriptions of the same type reuse these choices, with series favoring a consistent release and explaining what they are waiting for and how long. Existing subscriptions keep their rules.

#### A native app for Android phones and tablets

Browse libraries, search titles, subscribe and play discs, with subtitle switching, intro skipping and AI subtitle generation. Download the Android APK from this Release and connect it to your MovieClaw server.

#### Expand one series notification to see its downloads

Episode downloads combine into one notification card. Long-press on iPhone to inspect episodes and status, open the title or mute notifications for that series. Manage muted series on the web too. This takes effect after upgrading.

### More features and improvements

- **Easier settings navigation**: Server settings and account management are reorganized on the web and iPhone.
- **Smoother TV previews**: Apple TV hero trailers and card switching are more fluid.
- **Faster remote startup**: The Mac transcoder sends playable content earlier.
- **Recognizable subtitle tracks**: Web and Apple clients retain their original track titles.

### Fixed

- Playback requests opened from share links complete correctly.
- Naming templates can be saved without a title-ID placeholder.
- Single-episode images in series directories are not mistaken for the whole show's poster.
- Browsing a detail page alone is not recorded as active playback.

### Before you update

- This release migrates the database. To return to v0.32.0 or earlier, restore the automatic pre-update backup.
- Smart Selection is currently available on the web; Apple clients keep rule-based subscriptions.

Thanks to @anlan-home for the native Android client.

Full changes: [v0.32.0 → v0.33.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.32.0...v0.33.0).

## 简体中文

## v0.33.0：追更少配规则，看片多一种选择

这一版带来智能选种与 Android 原生 App。下载提醒可以按剧展开，网页和 iPhone 的设置也更好找了。

✅ 本版无需更新 Docker 镜像，管理员在「设置 → 更新与维护」一键更新即可。
使用 Apple 原生 App 的你，请同时更新到 0.6.0；使用 Mac 转码器时，请下载本版转码器更新。

### 本版亮点

#### 订阅先说偏好，选种交给服务器

不想为每部片配置规则？网页订阅时选择「智能选择」，分别设置电影和剧集的画质偏好、最低要求与等待耐心。
同类订阅会复用这些设置，剧集尽量跟随同一版本，并说明正在等什么、还会等多久。已有订阅继续使用原来的规则。

#### Android 也能用原生 App 看片

Android 手机和平板现在可以浏览媒体库、搜索影片、订阅追更和播放原盘，还能切换字幕、跳过片头与生成 AI 字幕。
在本版 Release 附件中下载 Android APK，连接你的 MovieClaw 服务器即可使用。

#### 一部剧的下载提醒，展开就能看清

一批剧集下载会合成一张通知卡。在 iPhone 上长按可查看集数和状态，并直接打开影片或关闭这部剧的提醒。
网页也能管理不再提醒的剧集，升级后自动生效。

### 其他新功能与改进

- **设置更好找**：网页和 iPhone 重新整理服务器设置与账号管理入口。
- **电视预览更顺畅**：Apple TV 首页大图预告与卡片切换更流畅。
- **远程转码起播更快**：Mac 转码器更早送出可播放的内容。
- **字幕更好认**：网页和 Apple 客户端保留字幕轨道原有标题。

### 修好了

- 用分享链接打开影片时，播放请求现在能正确完成。
- 命名模板不含影片编号时，以前无法保存，现在可以正常使用。
- 剧集目录里的单集图片不再被误用为整部剧的海报。
- 只浏览详情页时，不再被误记为正在播放。

### 升级须知

- 本版升级了数据库。如需回退到 v0.32.0 或更早版本，请用更新前自动生成的备份恢复。
- 智能选择入口目前在网页端；Apple 客户端继续使用原有规则订阅。

感谢 @anlan-home 带来 Android 原生客户端。

完整改动见 [v0.32.0 → v0.33.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.32.0...v0.33.0)。

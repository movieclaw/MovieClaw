## iPhone and iPad 0.12.4: A long reply stays readable when you press it

Pressing and holding a long assistant reply no longer squeezes the preview into a thin strip.

> ✅ Works with MovieClaw Server 0.28.0 or later, the same as 0.12.3. This build is the unsigned IPA at https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/tag/ios-v0.12.4. It is not on TestFlight.

### Fixed

- Open an AI conversation and press and hold the assistant’s reply. The preview keeps the bubble’s width. Its height stays within half of the message area, and the rest is clipped. A short reply still shows the whole bubble. **Copy (复制)** still copies the entire reply.

Full changes: [ios-v0.12.3 → ios-v0.12.4](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/ios-v0.12.3...ios-v0.12.4).

## 简体中文

## iPhone 与 iPad 0.12.4：长按长回复时，预览保持可读

长按很长的助手回复时，预览会被挤成一条窄条。这一版把预览保持在气泡原来的宽度里。

> ✅ 搭配 MovieClaw 服务器 0.28.0 或更新版本即可使用，与 0.12.3 相同。这一版是未签名 IPA：https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/tag/ios-v0.12.4。没有上传到 TestFlight。

### 修好了

- 打开一条 AI 会话，长按助手的回复。预览沿用气泡原来的宽度，高度不超过当前消息区域的一半，超出的部分裁掉。短回复仍能看全。「复制」复制的仍是整段回复。

完整改动：[ios-v0.12.3 → ios-v0.12.4](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/ios-v0.12.3...ios-v0.12.4)。

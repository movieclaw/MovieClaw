## iPhone and iPad 0.12.5: Back navigation keeps the composer clear

When you swipe back from an AI conversation, the composer now stays above the returning glass tab bar instead of slipping behind it.

> ✅ Works with MovieClaw Server 0.28.0 or later, the same as 0.12.4. This build is the unsigned IPA at https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/tag/ios-v0.12.5. It is not on TestFlight.

### Fixed

- During an edge swipe back from an AI conversation or the new-conversation page, the composer stays above the bottom glass tab bar while both are on screen. Cancelling the swipe returns the composer to its original position. Sidebars and top tab bars on wider iPad layouts are unchanged.

Full changes: [ios-v0.12.4 → ios-v0.12.5](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/ios-v0.12.4...ios-v0.12.5).

## 简体中文

## iPhone 与 iPad 0.12.5：返回时，输入框不会被标签栏盖住

从 AI 会话向右滑返回时，输入框会保持在重新出现的玻璃标签栏上方，不会滑到它后面。

> ✅ 搭配 MovieClaw 服务器 0.28.0 或更新版本即可使用，与 0.12.4 相同。这一版是未签名 IPA：https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/tag/ios-v0.12.5。没有上传到 TestFlight。

### 修好了

- 从 AI 会话或新会话页沿屏幕边缘滑动返回时，只要底部玻璃标签栏和输入框同时留在画面里，输入框就会让到标签栏上方；取消返回手势后会回到原来的位置。iPad 宽版式的侧栏和顶部标签栏保持不变。

完整改动：[ios-v0.12.4 → ios-v0.12.5](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/ios-v0.12.4...ios-v0.12.5)。

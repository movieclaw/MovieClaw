## v0.32.0: Watch on your Mac

MovieClaw now has a Mac app. Finding something to watch gets a new starting point too: browse movies and shows by genre.

> ✅ No Docker image update is required. Update from Settings → Update & Maintenance.

### Highlights

#### A real app for watching on your Mac

On a Mac you could only watch in the browser. The new Mac app browses your libraries, collections and favorites, searches titles and people, and plays with subtitle, audio and quality panels, skip intro, next episode and keyboard controls. Download https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.32.0/MovieClaw-macos-arm64.zip, unzip it and open the app.

- Needs an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later. The app is notarized by Apple.
- Needs MovieClaw server v0.32.0 or later. Older servers refuse its sign-in, and the app asks you to update the server.
- Sign in with a user name and password, or choose **QR code sign-in (扫码登录)** and approve it on iPhone under Settings → Devices (设备) or in a signed-in browser.
- Keep several accounts, even on different servers, and switch from the **Account (账号)** menu.
- It is made for watching: Discover, subscriptions, Reels and settings stay in the web app, and it gets no push notifications.

#### Not sure what to watch? Start with a genre

You no longer need a title in mind. Two new rows, **Browse movies by genre (按类型找电影)** and **Browse shows by genre (按类型找剧集)**, show a card for each genre with a still from the latest title added to it. Pick one to see every matching title across your libraries. The rows appear by themselves after My Libraries (我的媒体库) on the web Library page, iPhone and Mac.

- To hide them, use **Customize home (自定义首页)** on the Library page.
- Members only see genres from libraries they can access. Libraries excluded from the home page are left out.
- iPhone needs app 0.5.0. Older apps don't show these rows.

### New features

- **Reels plays more formats**: Reels (片段) on iPhone 0.5.0 now also plays Blu-ray folders, ISO images, DVD, TS and AVI. Older iPhone apps may fail to play these titles in Reels.
- **Clean up old devices**: Settings → Devices → **Clean up… (清理…)** lists devices unused for 7, 30 or 90 days (30 by default) and signs them out at once. Administrators can do it for everyone from the **All members (全部成员)** view. iPhone app 0.5.0 has it too.
- **What cleanup keeps**: The device you are using and connected transcoders stay. Jellyfin players such as Infuse are included and need to sign in again.
- **More naming placeholders**: Settings → Scrape & Organize (刮削与整理) → **Naming templates (命名模板)** adds English title, Douban ID, season name, video codec, HDR, bit depth, audio, site and original filename. Site and original filename are only filled for files MovieClaw imported itself, and original filenames are recorded from this version on.

### Improvements

- **Tidier file names**: A title repeated in one name, such as an original title equal to the title, is written once. Names over 200 bytes are shortened, keeping season, episode and specs.
- **Device list**: Each device shows an icon with an online dot. Rename and sign out (注销) moved into its ⋯ menu.
- **iPhone play button**: It names the episode, as in "继续 第 1 季第 3 集 · 46:56", and opening a show lands on the episode you are watching.
- **iPhone colors**: SDR video now has the same tone as in Infuse, instead of slightly bright, washed-out mid-tones.
- **iPhone Library page**: Reels (片段) is now the ▶ icon at the left of the top bar, next to ⋯.
- **iPhone player quality**: **Auto (自动)** is now called **Original (原画)**. It works the same.
- **Resource search**: **Search resources (搜索资源)** on a title and **Manual pick (手动选种)** on a subscription search only movies or only shows. Pick All (全部) to widen.
- **Library covers**: Once a library has four posters, its collage cover changes at most once a day.
- Plus 2 smaller improvements.

### Fixed

- After you finish a title and rewatch part of it, leaving and coming back continues from where you stopped.
- `{episode_title}` in naming templates was always empty. It now fills in the episode title.
- In Jellyfin players, libraries follow MovieClaw's order, the Collections (合集) view has a cover, and some posters that may not have shown now display.
- On iPhone 0.5.0, some DVD ISO images no longer play a few seconds of the menu instead of the film.
- On iPhone 0.5.0, titles switched to server transcoding may no longer stay stuck at 0:00.
- Plus 1 smaller fix.

### Before you update

- This release migrates the database. After an in-app update, you can return to v0.31.0 under Settings → Update & Maintenance → **Choose a version to roll back… (选择版本回退…)**. It restores the backup taken before the update; data added since is lost.
- If you updated by pulling the Docker image, there is no automatic backup. To return, stop the container, change the image tag to `movieclaw/movieclaw:v0.31.0`, restore your own backup of the data directory, then start it.
- Changes marked for iPhone need iPhone app 0.5.0: https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.32.0/MovieClaw-iOS-unsigned.ipa. Older apps keep working with this server, just without them.

Full changes: [v0.31.0 → v0.32.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.31.0...v0.32.0).

## 简体中文

## v0.32.0：在 Mac 上看片

MovieClaw 有了 Mac App。找片也多了一个起点：按类型浏览电影和剧集。

> ✅ 本版无需更新 Docker 镜像，在「设置 → 更新与维护」里更新即可。

### 本版亮点

#### Mac 上有了专门看片的 App

以前在 Mac 上只能用浏览器看。新的 Mac App 能浏览媒体库、合集和收藏，搜片名和影人；播放时有字幕、音轨和画质面板，能跳过片头、接着看下一集，也支持键盘操作。下载 https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.32.0/MovieClaw-macos-arm64.zip ，解压后打开即可。

- 需要 Apple 芯片的 Mac，macOS 26 或更新版本。App 已经过 Apple 公证。
- 需要 MovieClaw 服务器 v0.32.0 或更新版本。更旧的服务器会拒绝它登录，App 会提示先升级服务器。
- 可以用账号密码登录；也可以选「扫码登录」，在 iPhone「设置 → 设备」或已登录的浏览器里批准。
- 可以保存多个账号（不同服务器的也行），在「账号」菜单里切换。
- 它专门用来看片：发现、订阅、片段和设置仍在网页上，也收不到推送通知。

#### 不知道看什么，先挑一种类型

不用先想好片名。新增「按类型找电影」「按类型找剧集」两行，每种类型一张卡片，配上这个类型最近入库那部片的剧照。点开就是各个库里这个类型的全部作品。这两行会自动出现在「我的媒体库」之后，网页「媒体库」页、iPhone 和 Mac 都有。

- 不想要的话，在「媒体库」页的「自定义首页」里隐藏。
- 成员只会看到自己能访问的库里的类型。设成不在首页显示的库不计入。
- iPhone 需要 0.5.0 版 App，旧版 App 不显示这两行。

### 新功能

- **「片段」能放更多格式**：iPhone 0.5.0 的「片段」现在也能放蓝光原盘目录、ISO 镜像、DVD、TS 和 AVI。旧版 iPhone App 在「片段」里可能放不了这些片子。
- **清理长期没用的设备**：在「设置 → 设备 → 清理…」选 7、30 或 90 天（默认 30 天），先列出这段时间没用过的设备，再一次注销。管理员可以在「全部成员」视图里清理所有人的设备。iPhone App 0.5.0 也有。
- **清理会保留什么**：正在用的这台和连着的转码器不会被清理。Infuse 等 Jellyfin 播放器在清理范围内，被清掉后要重新登录。
- **更多命名占位符**：「设置 → 刮削与整理 → 命名模板」新增英文名、豆瓣 ID、季名、视频编码、HDR、位深、音轨、站点和原始文件名。站点和原始文件名只有 MovieClaw 自己入库的文件才有，原始文件名从这一版开始记录。

### 体验改进

- **文件名更整齐**：同一个名字里重复的片名（比如原名和片名相同）只写一次。超过 200 字节的名字会自动缩短，季集号和规格保留。
- **设备列表**：每台设备前有图标和在线标记。改名和注销收进了每行的 ⋯ 菜单。
- **iPhone 播放按钮**：写明是哪一集，比如「继续 第 1 季第 3 集 · 46:56」；打开剧集时直接落在正在看的那一集。
- **iPhone 画面色调**：SDR 视频的色调与 Infuse 一致，中间调不会再偏亮、发白。
- **iPhone 媒体库页**：「片段」改成顶栏最左边的 ▶ 图标，挨着 ⋯。
- **iPhone 播放画质**：「自动」改名为「原画」，作用不变。
- **搜索资源**：详情页的「搜索资源」和订阅的「手动选种」只搜电影或只搜剧集，想放宽可以点「全部」。
- **库封面**：库里满 4 张海报后，拼贴封面一天最多换一次。
- 以及 2 处细节改进。

### 修好了

- 看完的片子重看到一半，退出再进会接着这次的位置播放。
- 命名模板里的 `{episode_title}` 以前总是空的，现在会填入集名。
- 在 Jellyfin 播放器里，库的顺序与 MovieClaw 一致，「合集」视图有了封面，个别以前可能显示不出的海报也能显示了。
- iPhone 0.5.0 上，部分 DVD 镜像不会再只放几秒菜单、放不出正片。
- iPhone 0.5.0 上，改由服务器转码的片子起播时可能不会再停在 0:00。
- 以及 1 处细节修复。

### 升级须知

- 本版包含数据库迁移。通过应用内更新升级的，要回到 v0.31.0，可以在「设置 → 更新与维护 → 选择版本回退…」操作。它会恢复更新前自动生成的备份，此后新增的数据会丢失。
- 如果是拉取 Docker 镜像升级的，不会有自动备份。要回退：停止容器，把镜像标签改成 `movieclaw/movieclaw:v0.31.0`，恢复你自己备份的数据目录，再启动。
- 写明 iPhone 的改动需要 0.5.0 版 iPhone App：https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.32.0/MovieClaw-iOS-unsigned.ipa 。旧版 App 仍能连这台服务器，只是没有这些改动。

完整变更：[v0.31.0 → v0.32.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.31.0...v0.32.0)。

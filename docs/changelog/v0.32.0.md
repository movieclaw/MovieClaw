## v0.32.0: A native place to watch on your Mac

A native Mac app joins genre browsing for movies and series. Apple TV hero artwork previews clips, and discs and more source formats gain previews and Clips.

✅ No Docker image update is required. Administrators can update from **Settings → Update & Maintenance**.
Update the native app to **0.5.0** as well.

### Highlights

#### Your own viewing window on Mac

Browse your library, search titles and people, manage multiple accounts, choose subtitles, audio and quality, skip intros and continue to the next episode. The window follows the picture size and common controls support the keyboard. Download the Mac app from this Release and connect it to your MovieClaw server.

#### Pick a genre when you do not know what to watch

Browse Movies by Genre and Browse Series by Genre use library stills to present action, comedy, science fiction and other genres. Open one to browse matching titles on the web, iPhone, Apple TV or Mac.

#### Preview a title right inside the hero

Apple TV home and detail heroes play an excerpt after you pause on them. Continue Watching can recap what you watched last. Hero previews and Clips now support Blu-ray discs, disc images, DVD, TS and AVI sources automatically after upgrading.

### More features and improvements

- **Clearer devices**: Settings → Devices groups by type and can remove long-unused devices in batches.
- **Richer filenames**: Naming templates add placeholders, including episode titles.
- **Clearer continuation**: The iPhone detail Play button identifies the episode it will start.
- **Stable library covers**: Collage covers change at most once a day instead of frequently during browsing.

### Fixed

- Rewatching a completed title and stopping halfway now resumes from the new position.
- Resource Search from details and manual torrent selection narrows results to movies or series.
- Jellyfin-compatible clients display library order and collection covers correctly.
- Episode-title placeholders now contain the title instead of staying empty.

### Before you update

- This release migrates the database. To return to v0.31.0 or earlier, restore the automatic pre-update backup.

Full changes: [v0.31.0 → v0.32.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.31.0...v0.32.0).

## 简体中文

## v0.32.0：在 Mac 上，也能自在看片

这一版带来 Mac 原生 App，也让找片多了一个入口：按类型浏览电影和剧集。
Apple TV 大图能直接预览片段，原盘和更多格式的片源也加入了预告与「片段」。

✅ 本版无需更新 Docker 镜像，管理员在「设置 → 更新与维护」一键更新即可。
使用原生 App 的你，请同时更新到 0.5.0。

### 本版亮点

#### Mac 上有了自己的看片窗口

在电脑上看片，现在可以用 Mac 原生 App：浏览媒体库、搜索影片和影人、管理多个账号，
播放时切字幕、音轨与画质，也能跳过片头、接着看下一集。窗口随画面调整，常用操作支持键盘。
在本版 Release 附件中下载 Mac App，连接你的 MovieClaw 服务器即可开始。

#### 不知道看什么，先挑一种类型

不必先想好片名。首页新增「按类型找电影」「按类型找剧集」，用库里的剧照呈现动作、喜剧、
科幻等类型，点开就是对应的影片墙。网页、iPhone、Apple TV 和 Mac 都能按类型找片。

#### 大图里的片子，可以先看一小段

Apple TV 首页和详情页的大图停留后会原地播放片段，「接着看」还能回顾上次看到的内容。
大图预告与「片段」现在也支持蓝光原盘、光盘镜像、DVD、TS 和 AVI 片源，升级后自动生效。

### 其他新功能与改进

- **设备列表更好认**：在「设置 → 设备」按类型查看登录设备，也能批量清理长期没用的设备。
- **文件命名更丰富**：命名模板开放更多占位符，剧集标题也能写进文件名。
- **继续播放更明确**：iPhone 影片页的播放按钮会写明将播放哪一集。
- **库封面更稳定**：拼贴封面一天最多换一次，浏览时不再频繁变图。

### 修好了

- 看完的影片重新看到一半，退出后再进会接着这次的位置播放。
- 详情页和手动选种的「搜索资源」会按电影、剧集收窄结果。
- 用 Jellyfin 客户端浏览时，库顺序和合集封面现在能正确显示。
- 命名模板中的剧集标题以前总是空的，现在会填入对应标题。

### 升级须知

- 本版升级了数据库。如需回退到 v0.31.0 或更早版本，请用更新前自动生成的备份恢复。

完整改动见 [v0.31.0 → v0.32.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.31.0...v0.32.0)。

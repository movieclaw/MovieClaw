## iPhone and iPad 0.8.0: Your library, even without internet

Download films and episodes before you leave, watch your local copies without reaching the server, and sync your viewing progress when you return.

> **Offline downloads require MovieClaw Server 0.38.0 or later.** You can update the app first: download controls stay hidden on older servers, and online playback remains available.

### Highlights

#### Download tonight, watch on tomorrow's journey

Open a film or series, choose **Download** from its menu, select the original or a smaller quality, then confirm with **Download**. For a series, choose the current episode, unwatched episodes or all episodes; episodes already downloaded or downloading are skipped. Each choice shows the total size, and converted sizes use ≈ to indicate an estimate.

Downloads continue in the background, support pause and resume, and use Wi-Fi by default. To allow mobile data, open **Me → Downloads → Download settings**. Subtitle files, ASS fonts and artwork are saved alongside the video.

#### Keep watching when your server is out of reach

When the app cannot reach your server, you can enter offline mode and browse the titles stored on this device. Local copies play directly, including episode selection, seeking, audio and subtitle controls, and resume points. Offline viewing records sync after reconnection; online local playback also checks for a newer server resume point.

#### Manage downloads in one place

The home page shows one download summary with the title and overall progress. Tap it, or open **Me → Downloads**, to see each active episode, pause or cancel tasks, open completed titles and delete watched downloads. A title's local copies also appear in its file section; episode cards show download status. The capacity bar refreshes when downloads finish or files are deleted.

### More features and improvements

- **Clear waiting states**: A queued conversion shows how many jobs are ahead or whether it is waiting for a transcoder, without calling normal waiting an interrupted download.
- **Less waiting before a series download**: Quality choices and total sizes are fetched together when the download sheet opens.
- **Compact title actions**: Download, collection and sharing actions are grouped in the title menu, with confirmations anchored to the action you chose.
- **Separate accounts**: Local copies stay associated with their server and account. Signing out lets you choose whether to keep or delete them.

### Before you update

- Disc folders and ISO images cannot be downloaded. Converted copies contain one audio track and omit image-based subtitles; original copies retain the tracks in the source file.
- Force-quitting the app stops background downloads until you open it again. New downloads and synchronization need a reachable server.
- Download permission can prevent new downloads; copies already on the device remain playable.

## 简体中文

## iPhone 与 iPad 0.8.0：没网，也能看自己的片库

出发前把电影和剧集下好，连不上服务器时播放本机副本；重新联网后，观看进度会同步回来。

> **离线下载需要 MovieClaw 服务器 0.38.0 或更新版本。** 可以先更新 App：旧服务器上下载入口会隐藏，在线播放仍可使用。

### 本版亮点

#### 今晚下好，明天路上看

打开电影或剧集，在菜单里选「下载」，选择原画或更小的画质，再点「下载」确认。剧集可选当前一集、未看的集或全部，已下载或正在下载的集不会重复下载。每个选项会显示整批大小，转码大小用「≈」表示估算。

下载可在后台继续，支持暂停和续传，默认只用 Wi-Fi。要允许蜂窝网络，请打开「我的 → 下载 → 下载设置」。字幕文件、ASS 字体和图片会与视频一起保存。

#### 连不上家，也能接着看

App 连不上服务器时，可以进入离线模式，浏览这台设备上已经下载的片。本机副本直接播放，支持选集、拖动、音轨与字幕切换，以及续播点。离线观看记录在重新联网后同步；在线时播放本机副本，也会检查服务器有没有更新的续播点。

#### 下载都在一个地方管理

首页用一条汇总提示显示正在下载的片与总进度。点提示条，或打开「我的 → 下载」，可查看每一集的下载状态、暂停或取消任务、打开已下载条目，以及删除已经看完的下载。影片详情的文件区也能找到本机副本，分集卡会显示下载状态。容量条在下完或删除文件后更新。

### 其他新功能与改进

- **排队状态更清楚**：转码排队时显示前面还有几个任务，或正在等待转码器，不把正常等待写成下载中断。
- **整部剧选档更快**：打开下载面板时一次获取整批画质与总大小。
- **影片操作更紧凑**：下载、合集和分享收进影片菜单，确认提示出现在对应操作旁。
- **不同账号各自保存**：本机副本属于对应的服务器与账号；退出登录时可选择保留或删除。

### 升级须知

- 原盘目录与 ISO 暂不能下载。转码副本只保留一条音轨，不含图形字幕；原画副本保留源文件内的轨道。
- 强退 App 会中止后台下载，重新打开后继续；新下载与同步需要能连上服务器。
- 下载权限可以阻止新下载，已经下到设备上的副本仍可播放。

完整改动见 [ios-v0.7.0 → ios-v0.8.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/ios-v0.7.0...ios-v0.8.0)。

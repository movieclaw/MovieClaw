## v0.31.0: Find it, and hear when it arrives

Library search understands pinyin, initials and Traditional Chinese, with consistent results on the web, iPhone and Apple TV. The iPhone app adds notifications for new episodes and imports, and an Apple TV app arrives.

> ⚠️ **A Docker image update is required.** Dependencies have changed. Run `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d` in your deployment directory. Without the new image, Update & Maintenance cannot perform an in-app update.
> Update the iPhone app to **0.4.0** as well.

### Highlights

#### A few letters can find the Chinese title you mean

Search using full pinyin, initials such as `st` or `lyt`, mixed pinyin and Chinese, or Traditional Chinese. Actors and directors have their own row leading to works in your library. Non-title matches explain why they appear, such as the matching actor. Web, iPhone and Apple TV share ranking and suggestions as you type. This applies automatically after upgrading.

#### Know when a new film or episode arrives

After connecting MovieClaw Cloud, the iPhone app receives end-to-end encrypted notifications for subscribed episodes, new content in chosen libraries, completed manual downloads and new device logins. Administrators also receive version reminders. An administrator connects the account in Settings → MovieClaw Cloud, then each person chooses notifications in Settings → Notifications. Sideloaded apps cannot receive push notifications.

#### MovieClaw on Apple TV

A TV interface with focus-driven hero artwork, two-part detail pages, people, discovery and subscriptions. Playback supports intro skipping and automatic next episodes. Sign in by scanning the TV's pairing code from the iPhone app's Me page and approving it.

### More features and improvements

- **Sharper images**: Posters and stills use originals by default, with locally saved portraits and adjustable artwork quality in Scraping & Organization.
- **Fanart.tv artwork**: Add your own key for more poster, backdrop and title-logo choices.
- **A separate boosting downloader**: Choose one per tracker instead of sharing the subscription downloader.
- **New trackers**: SunnyPT and UBits.
- **Faster intro and credit detection**: Concurrency follows machine capacity, slowing rather than stopping entirely during playback.
- **Lighter metadata refreshes**: Avoid downloading every image again; lower-quality choices resize local artwork.
- **Hide Continue Watching on iPhone**: Dismiss it with ✕; it returns when that device plays again.

### Fixed

- The iPhone app can connect using an HTTP domain without a -1022 login failure.
- System logs no longer record tracker passkeys, along with other detail fixes.

### Before you update

- This release migrates the database. To return to v0.30.0 or earlier, restore the automatic pre-update backup.

Full changes: [v0.30.0 → v0.31.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/movieclaw/compare/v0.30.0...v0.31.0).

## 简体中文

## v0.31.0：搜得到，也收得到

这一版让找片和收消息都省事了：媒体库搜索认拼音、首字母和繁体，网页、iPhone、Apple TV 搜出来一个样；
iPhone App 能收到追的剧更新、新片入库等推送。Apple TV 版也随这一版到来。

> ⚠️ **需要你动手**：本版包含依赖变化，**需升级 Docker 镜像**：在部署目录执行
> `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`。不升级的话，「设置 → 更新与维护」会提示需要新镜像，无法一键更新。
> iPhone App 请同时更新到 0.4.0。

### 本版亮点

#### 记不清片名，打几个字母也能搜到

想找《三体》却懒得切中文输入法，或者只记得演员名字的拼音，以前常常搜不到。现在搜索框里
打全拼、首字母（如「st」「lyt」）、中文拼音混输或繁体都能命中，演员和导演单独成一行，
点进去就是他在你库里的全部作品；不是按片名命中的结果会注明原因，比如「演员：史蒂芬·朗」。
网页、iPhone、Apple TV 用的是同一套排序，输入时就有联想。升级后自动生效。

#### 新片入库，手机上马上知道

以前只能开着网页或 IM 机器人等消息。现在连接 MovieClaw Cloud 后，iPhone App 能收到端到端加密的推送：
订阅的剧更新了、关心的库有新片、你点的下载已入库、新设备登录了你的账号，管理员还会收到新版本提醒。
管理员先在「设置 → MovieClaw Cloud」连接账号，每个人再到「设置 → 通知」选想收的。侧载安装的 App 收不到推送。

#### Apple TV 版

为电视重新设计的界面：焦点驱动的大图首页、两截式详情页、影人页、发现与订阅，
支持跳过片头和自动下一集，登录时电视上显示配对码，用 iPhone「我的」页扫码批准即可。

### 其他新功能与改进

- **图片更清晰**：海报、剧照默认用原图，演员头像存到本地；「设置 → 刮削与整理」里可选本地图片画质。
- **Fanart.tv 图片来源**：在「设置 → 刮削与整理」填入自己的 Key，海报、背景、片名 Logo 有更多可选。
- **刷流单独选下载器**：为站点开启刷流时可指定一台下载器，不再和订阅抢同一台。
- **新增站点**：SunnyPT、UBits。
- **片头片尾识别更快**：按机器配置并行识别，有人看片时放慢而不是整个暂停。
- **刷新元数据更省**：手动刷新不再把全部图片重下一遍，降低画质也直接用本地图缩小。
- **「接着看」可以隐藏**：iPhone 上点 ✕ 收起，这台设备再播时重新出现。

### 修好了

- iPhone App 用「http + 域名」连接服务器时报 -1022 登录不上，现在不会了。
- 以及系统日志不再记下站点的 passkey 等细节修复。

### 升级须知

- 本版升级了数据库。之后如需回退到 v0.30.0 或更早版本，请用更新前自动生成的备份恢复。

完整改动见 [v0.30.0 → v0.31.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/movieclaw/compare/v0.30.0...v0.31.0)。

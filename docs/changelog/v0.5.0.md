## v0.5.0: Connect your library to the player you already use

Jellyfin compatibility lets VidHub, Infuse and other compatible players connect directly to MovieClaw. Download and import notifications now include artwork.

> ⚠️ **A Docker image update is required.** New image-processing dependencies are included. Pull the new image and recreate the container.
> Docker Compose users can also map `7359/udp` to enable automatic player discovery on the local network.

### Highlights

#### Watch your library in VidHub or Infuse

Add your MovieClaw address as a Jellyfin server to browse libraries, titles and episodes, then play them directly. Progress, watched status and favorites are stored on the server for multiple devices. strm content redirects to its cloud-drive playback URL.

#### Recognize a download notification at a glance

Telegram and Discord notifications include a backdrop or poster, with the year, quality, freeleech status and seeder count. If the image cannot be sent, a text notification is used instead.

### More features and improvements

- **Explore cast and directors**: Player detail pages show people and portraits, with links to their related works in your library.
- **Automatic library covers**: Recently added posters form a shared cover for the web interface and players.
- **Clearer notification settings**: Account connections and notification content are managed separately. WeChat shows a pairing code when you open its dialog.

### Fixed

- WeChat subscription notifications retain and reuse the paired user's conversation information, including after a restart, instead of disappearing silently.

### Before you update

- Send the WeChat bot a message after pairing to establish a conversation for outgoing notifications.
- This release migrates the database. Restore the automatic pre-update backup when rolling back across versions.

Full changes: [v0.4.0 → v0.5.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.4.0...v0.5.0).

## 简体中文

## v0.5.0：用你熟悉的播放器，连接自己的片库

这一版带来 Jellyfin 兼容接口。VidHub、Infuse 等播放器可以直接连接 MovieClaw，下载与入库通知也有了影片配图。

> ⚠️ **本版需要更新 Docker 镜像**：新增图片处理依赖，请先拉取新镜像并重建容器。
> Docker Compose 用户还可补上 `7359/udp` 端口映射，启用局域网播放器自动发现。

### 本版亮点

#### 片库直接连到 VidHub、Infuse

在支持 Jellyfin 的播放器里，把 MovieClaw 地址作为服务器添加，就能浏览媒体库、查看影片和分集、直接播放。观看进度、已看与收藏保存在服务器，多台设备可以接着看；strm 网盘内容直接跳转到云盘地址播放。

#### 下载提醒，也能一眼认出是哪部片

Telegram、Discord 的订阅通知带上横版剧照或海报，文字补齐年份、画质、免费状态和做种数。图片发送失败会退回文字提醒。

### 其他新功能与改进

- **演员和导演能点开看**：播放器详情页展示演职员与头像，可继续浏览库内相关作品。
- **媒体库封面自动拼贴**：用最近入库的作品海报生成封面，网页和播放器共用。
- **消息推送设置更好找**：账号接入与推送内容分开管理，微信绑定在打开弹窗后才出二维码。

### 修好了

- 微信订阅通知以前可能无声丢失，现在会保存并复用绑定人的会话信息，重启也能继续推送。

### 升级须知

- 微信绑定后仍需先给机器人发一条消息，建立可用于主动推送的会话。
- 本版升级了数据库。如需跨版本回退，请用更新前自动生成的备份恢复。

完整改动见 [v0.4.0 → v0.5.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.4.0...v0.5.0)。

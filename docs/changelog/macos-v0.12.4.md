## Mac 0.12.4: The season shows when the next episode airs

On a series that is still airing, the detail page shows an air schedule above the episodes. Episodes that have not aired are no longer greyed out as missing.

> ✅ Works with MovieClaw Server 0.31.0 or later, the same as 0.12.3. The air schedule needs Server 0.44.0; on an older server the strip stays hidden and unaired episodes stay greyed out as missing. Download from https://movieclaw.io/en/download and replace the old app. The direct-download edition updates itself.

### Highlights

#### See the next air date above the episodes

Open a series whose current season still has an air date today or later. Above the episode row, one line names what airs today or next, the cadence, and how the episode is expected to arrive. A narrower window drops the finale date first, then the cadence. Click the line. A panel opens from it, the same way **All episodes** does. Click outside or press Esc to close it. The left side shows how many episodes are left, **Find this episode** for a gap, and **Follow new episodes**. The right side is the days, grouped so you can see this week, next week, and later.

An episode that has not aired no longer looks like a missing file. Its still shows the air date. An episode that has aired and is not in the library stays missing and cannot be played.

**Follow new episodes** uses the subscription already on the server. If smart selection has never been saved, the panel says **Finish setting up smart selection on your phone or the web first.** A member who cannot edit that setup sees the existing notice to ask an administrator.

Full changes: [macos-v0.12.3 → macos-v0.12.4](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/macos-v0.12.3...macos-v0.12.4).

## 简体中文

## Mac 0.12.4：这一季会写明下一集什么时候播

还在播出的剧，详情页在分集上面显示播出日历。还没播的集不再被当成缺集置灰。

> ✅ 搭配 MovieClaw 服务器 0.31.0 或更新版本即可使用，与 0.12.3 相同。播出日历需要服务器 0.44.0；更旧的服务器上这条不出现，还没播的集仍按缺集置灰。从 https://movieclaw.io/zh/download 下载，替换旧版 App。官网直装版会自己更新。

### 本版亮点

#### 分集上面能看到下一次播出

打开一部本季还有今天或以后播出日期的剧。分集横排上面有一行，写今天或下次更新哪几集、更新节奏，以及预计什么时候入库。窗口窄了先省完结日，再省节奏。点这一行，从这里弹出面板，和「全部分集」一样。点外面或按 Esc 关掉。左边是还剩几集、缺了的集可以「补这一集」，以及「订阅追更」。右边按天排列，能看出本周、下周和之后。

还没播的集不再看起来像缺了文件。剧照位换成播出日。已经播出、库里却没有的集仍是缺集，不能点播。

「订阅追更」用的是服务器上已有的订阅。智能选择还没保存过时，面板写「首次订阅要先在手机或网页上完成智能设置」。不能改这项设置的成员，仍看到请管理员先完成的说明。

完整改动：[macos-v0.12.3 → macos-v0.12.4](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/macos-v0.12.3...macos-v0.12.4)。

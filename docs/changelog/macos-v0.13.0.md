## Mac 0.13.0: Keep the player open while choosing an episode

Series now have an episode sidebar inside the player. It keeps the picture visible while you inspect the season, switch to another available episode, or move between seasons.

> ✅ Works with MovieClaw Server 0.31.0 or later, the same as 0.12.7. Changing seasons inside the player requires Server 0.50.0 or later; older supported servers still show the current season. Download from https://movieclaw.io/en/download and replace the old app. The direct-download edition updates itself.

### Highlights

#### Choose the next episode beside the picture

Select **Episodes** in the player controls or press **E**. In a wide window, the sidebar sits beside a smaller picture and can stay open while you watch; in a narrow window it appears over the picture. The current episode shows its synopsis and progress, missing episodes remain visible but unavailable, and choosing another episode starts it directly.

Full changes: [macos-v0.12.7 → macos-v0.13.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/macos-v0.12.7...macos-v0.13.0).

## 简体中文

## Mac 0.13.0：播放器不关，也能挑下一集

剧集播放器现在有自己的选集侧栏。查看这一季、切换到另一集或换季时，画面仍留在眼前。

> ✅ 搭配 MovieClaw 服务器 0.31.0 或更新版本即可使用，与 0.12.7 相同。在播放器里跨季切换需要服务器 0.50.0 或更新版本；更早但仍受支持的服务器仍会显示当前季。从 https://movieclaw.io/zh/download 下载并替换旧版 App。官网直装版会自己更新。

### 本版亮点

#### 在画面旁边选下一集

点播放器控制面板里的「选集」，或按 **E**。宽窗口中，侧栏会和缩小后的画面并排，可以边看边开着；窄窗口中则浮在画面上。正在播放的一集会显示简介和进度，缺集仍会列出但不能点；选择另一集后会直接开始播放。

完整改动：[macos-v0.12.7 → macos-v0.13.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/macos-v0.12.7...macos-v0.13.0)。

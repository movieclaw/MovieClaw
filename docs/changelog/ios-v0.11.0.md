## iPhone and iPad 0.11.0: Your language, your picture

Use MovieClaw in English or Chinese and adjust the picture to suit your screen.

- The interface now supports Simplified Chinese and English. The language entry opens the system's per-app language settings; Server 0.43.0 adds matching server messages.
- Adjust video display modes and restore the original presentation. Subtitles follow the picture, including after rotation while paused.
- Rule groups can exclude Dolby Vision Profile 5 with **DV P5**; use Server 0.43.0 for this rule.

Full changes: [ios-v0.10.0 → ios-v0.11.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/ios-v0.10.0...ios-v0.11.0).

## 简体中文

## iPhone 与 iPad 0.11.0：语言与画面随你选

界面新增英语，画面与字幕布局更贴合你的屏幕。

- 界面新增英语，与简体中文一起随系统选择；应用内「语言」入口可打开系统的单独 App 语言设置。服务器提示的对应语言需配合服务器 0.43.0。
- 可调整画面显示模式并恢复原样，字幕随画面布局；改进暂停后旋转屏幕的字幕位置。
- 规则组新增「DV P5」选项，可排除杜比视界 Profile 5，需配合服务器 0.43.0。

完整改动：[ios-v0.10.0 → ios-v0.11.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/ios-v0.10.0...ios-v0.11.0)。

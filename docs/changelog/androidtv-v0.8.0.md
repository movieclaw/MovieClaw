## Android TV 0.8.0: Choose an episode from the player

Series now have an episode shelf inside the player. The remote can open it, move through available episodes, and change seasons without returning to the detail page. The Android TV interface remains Chinese.

> ✅ Works with MovieClaw Server 0.28.0 or later, the same as 0.7.5. Changing seasons inside the player requires Server 0.50.0 or later; older supported servers still show the current season. Install from https://movieclaw.io/download/androidtv.apk

### Highlights

#### Browse episodes without leaving the picture

Choose **选集** above the progress bar to open the still-image shelf. Focus starts on the episode currently playing; move across the shelf and press the remote to switch, or move up to choose another season. Missing episodes remain visible and are skipped by focus.

This shelf replaces the previous full-screen episode-number grid. Press back or down to close it without leaving playback.

Full changes: [androidtv-v0.7.5 → androidtv-v0.8.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/androidtv-v0.7.5...androidtv-v0.8.0).

## 简体中文

## Android TV 0.8.0：直接从播放器选集

剧集播放器现在有自己的分集剧照排。用遥控器就能打开、切换可播放的集数，也能换季，不必先退回详情页。Android TV 界面仍是中文。

> ✅ 搭配 MovieClaw 服务器 0.28.0 或更新版本即可使用，与 0.7.5 相同。在播放器里跨季切换需要服务器 0.50.0 或更新版本；更早但仍受支持的服务器仍会显示当前季。从 https://movieclaw.io/download/androidtv.apk 安装。

### 本版亮点

#### 不离开画面，也能浏览分集

选择进度条上方的「选集」，会打开分集剧照排。焦点先落在正在播放的一集；左右移动后按遥控器即可换集，继续向上可以选择其他季。缺集仍会列出，焦点会自动跳过。

这个剧照排替代了原来的全屏集号宫格。按返回键或下键可以收起，不会退出播放。

完整改动：[androidtv-v0.7.5 → androidtv-v0.8.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/androidtv-v0.7.5...androidtv-v0.8.0)。

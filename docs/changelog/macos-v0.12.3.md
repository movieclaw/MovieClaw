## Mac 0.12.3: Arrow keys stay on the picture

Seeking with the arrow keys no longer opens the control bar. When nothing is next, the player closes and leaves you on that title.

> ✅ Works with MovieClaw Server 0.31.0 or later, the same as 0.12.1. Download from https://movieclaw.io/en/download and replace the old app. The direct-download edition updates itself.

### Fixed

- Pressing the left or right arrow to move 10 seconds only flashes the icon in the center of the picture. It no longer opens the bottom control bar or the back button. The ±10 second buttons on the control bar still behave as before: the bar is already open, and the hide timer starts again.
- When a movie or the last episode of the season ends, and there is no next episode, the player closes. If you started from Home, a poster wall, or search, that title is placed on top, and Back returns to the page you came from. If you were already on the title, a second copy is not added. A next episode still shows **Up next** and counts down. A clip that reaches the end, and shared playback, only close the player.

Full changes: [macos-v0.12.1 → macos-v0.12.3](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/macos-v0.12.1...macos-v0.12.3).

## 简体中文

## Mac 0.12.3：方向键跳转不唤出控制栏

用方向键前进或后退时，底部控制栏不再弹出来。没有下一集时，播放器关掉，落在这部片上。

> ✅ 搭配 MovieClaw 服务器 0.31.0 或更新版本即可使用，与 0.12.1 相同。从 https://movieclaw.io/zh/download 下载，替换旧版 App。官网直装版会自己更新。

### 修好了

- 按 ← 或 → 前进、后退 10 秒时，只在画面正中闪一下图标，不再弹出底部控制栏和返回键。点控制栏上的 ±10 秒按钮仍和以前一样：控制栏本来就在，隐藏计时重新开始。
- 电影或本季最后一集放完，又没有下一集时，播放器会关掉。从首页、海报墙或搜索起播的，这部片压在原来的页面上，返回回到来的地方。本来就在这部片上起播的，不会再压一层。有下一集时仍显示「即将播放」并倒计时。片段放到终点，以及分享播放，只关掉播放器。

完整改动：[macos-v0.12.1 → macos-v0.12.3](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/macos-v0.12.1...macos-v0.12.3)。

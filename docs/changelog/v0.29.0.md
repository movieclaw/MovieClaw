## v0.29.0: Quick starts, even away from home

This release is about watching on iPhone outside your home network: playback starts and seeks much faster on slow connections, and the app suggests a lower quality sooner when your connection can't keep up. The iPhone app also gains Reels, a vertical feed of clips from your own library, and default audio and subtitles now follow the film and the library.

> ✅ No Docker image update is required. Update from Settings → Update & Maintenance.
> ⚠️ **Update the server before the iPhone app 0.2.0.** On a v0.28.0 server, iPhone 0.2.0 can't open **Change artwork (更换图片)**, and manual downloads that ask you to confirm the title fail. The earlier 0.1.0 app keeps working with this server.

### Highlights

#### Watch 4K on your phone without the long wait

Streaming a high-bitrate file over mobile data used to mean a long wait before the first frame, and another one after every seek. With iPhone 0.2.0, playback starts while data is still arriving: MKV files with many subtitle tracks no longer download a large index first, and seeking past the buffer starts reading right where you land. Resuming a title, even after reopening the app, is much faster too. It applies automatically after you update the server and the app.

- The lower-quality suggestion now also appears after a single 8-second wait (for the first frame, after a seek, or mid-playback) when your connection is slower than the file. It still asks at most once per playback and never switches on its own.
- The faster MKV start helps from the second play of a file and for the next episode: the server prepares a small index the first time. It shows up in Settings → Update & Maintenance → Cache Management as **MKV compact index (MKV 精简索引)**.
- The app keeps recently played data on the phone across launches, up to 512 MB, and removes it after 30 days unused. iOS can also reclaim it when storage runs low.

#### Reels: scroll through your own library

Not sure what to watch? In the iPhone app, tap **▶ Reels (片段)** at the top right of the Media library tab and swipe up through highlights picked from your movies and shows. Tap **Details (详情)** to open the title, or **Full screen (全屏观看)** and then **Watch full title (看全片)** to keep going. Filter by movie or show, genre, decade, country, minimum rating, length, or unwatched only.

- Requires iPhone 0.2.0 and this server version. Members see clips only from libraries they can access.
- MKV and MP4 movies and shows only; Blu-ray, DVD, ISO, TS and cloud-drive strm items are skipped. A show's clip comes from episode 2 of its first season.
- Quality defaults to original at home and 720p away from home or on mobile data; switch it with the quality button. On mobile data the app asks before opening Reels.
- Scrolling clips doesn't add to watch history. Only **Watched (已看)** or continuing with the full title does.

#### Audio and subtitles that pick themselves

Default tracks used to follow whatever the file flagged, so you often got a dub or had to turn subtitles on yourself. Now the default audio is the film's original language, and the default subtitles follow your library's metadata language: a foreign film in a Chinese library opens Chinese subtitles. Only tracks you change by hand are remembered, so an automatic pick no longer sticks to a whole series. It applies automatically on the web and in iPhone 0.2.0; Jellyfin clients such as Infuse may follow it too.

- The audio and subtitle rows on a title's page mark the track that will play for you and say why.
- Commentary and audio-description tracks are never picked automatically.
- On upgrade, saved choices that were just the old automatic pick are cleared, so they follow the new defaults. Choices you made yourself stay.

### New features

- **Title logos**: Title pages on the web (default theme) and in the iPhone app show the title's logo, and Jellyfin clients receive it too. On the web, pick another one in the title's ⋯ → **Change artwork** → **Logo (徽标)**. Existing titles get their logo at the next metadata refresh, and libraries that write artwork into media folders also write `clearlogo.png`.
- **Confirm the title for manual downloads**: When a search-result download isn't recognized, you can search for the title and pick it on the web or iPhone. The library is then chosen automatically, as for recognized downloads.
- **Switch accounts on iPhone**: Long-press the avatar tab to switch accounts, or double-tap it to go back to the previous one.
- **Continue watching bar on iPhone**: On iOS 26.1 or later, the next title to continue sits above the tab bar; tap to play, or long-press to hide it for now.

### Improvements

- **iPhone tabs**: The order is now Media library, Subscriptions, Discover, Activity, Me, and every account opens on Media library. Administrators used to start on Discover.
- **iPhone Me page**: Settings is now **Server settings (服务器设置)**. **About MovieClaw (关于 MovieClaw)** moved to the bottom of Server settings, and **Log out (退出登录)** to the bottom of your profile page.
- **Faster large libraries**: Opening **More filters (更多筛选)** and a library page are much faster on libraries with many files. In our test, a library with 18,000 files went from about two minutes to under a second.
- **AI subtitles respond at once**: Starting AI subtitles responds right away, and stopping takes effect within seconds. Reading the subtitle track shows its own progress, model errors say what went wrong, and after a temporary outage the job retries from where it stopped.
- **Smaller touches**: On iPhone, the side buttons show the same volume indicator as swiping during playback, and large artwork on Discover and Subscriptions no longer shows bare edges. The web Activity page on desktop (default theme) uses the same overview and sub-pages as the app and phone.

### Fixed

- With the Mac transcoder on ffmpeg 7.1, GPU transcodes that had to scale the picture down always failed.
- Season folders with extra text in the name, like `Season 4-第 4 季-(2007)`, were treated as separate titles. When a season couldn't be determined, episodes were silently filed under Specials; now they wait in unrecognized items so you can pick the season.
- After a quality upgrade, the same download could be copied into the library a second time.
- In the browser, a title with a Mandarin DTS track and an English AC3 track could switch to English to avoid transcoding.
- In the iPhone player, a faint oval no longer shows in the middle of dark scenes.

### For administrators

- MovieClaw pages can no longer be shown inside an iframe on another site, such as a dashboard. The API description file is no longer public; set `APP_ENV=local` to bring it back.
- The first time anyone opens Reels after the server starts, the server reads the index of one file per movie or show in the background, once.

### Before you update

- **Generate chapters (生成章节) is now off by default, and turned off for every existing library.** While it's off, title pages show no chapter row and Jellyfin clients such as Infuse get no chapter images; chapters built into the file still work. To turn it back on (administrators), go to Media library → the gear button (管理媒体库) → ⋯ next to the library → Edit library → Scan & monitoring → **Generate chapters**. Images already generated are kept, and missing ones are generated in the background.
- The subtitle **Outline (描边)** option is gone on the web and iPhone. Without a background, subtitles are white with a soft shadow.
- This release migrates the database. In-app update backs it up first; to return to v0.28.0, use Settings → Update & Maintenance → **Choose a version to roll back to… (选择版本回退…)**, which restores that backup, so changes made after the update are lost. If you update by pulling the Docker image, stop the container and back up the data directory first; to return, change the image tag back to `v0.28.0` and restore that backup.
- Changes marked for iPhone need app 0.2.0: https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.29.0/MovieClaw-iOS-unsigned.ipa

## 简体中文

## v0.29.0：在外面也能秒开

这一版主攻 iPhone App 在外网的播放：慢网络下起播、拖进度条都快了很多，网速跟不上时会更早提示你换低画质。App 还多了「片段」，在自己的片库里竖着滑就能刷片；默认音轨、字幕也改成跟着影片和媒体库走。

> ✅ 本版无需更新 Docker 镜像，在「设置 → 更新与维护」一键更新即可。
> ⚠️ **请先升级服务器，再装 iPhone App 0.2.0。** iPhone 0.2.0 连 v0.28.0 服务器时，打不开「更换图片」，需要确认作品的手动下载也会失败。旧的 0.1.0 版 App 连本版服务器照常可用。

### 本版亮点

#### 在外面用手机看 4K，也不用干等

以前出门用蜂窝网络看高码率原片，起播要等很久，拖一下进度又要等。现在用 iPhone 0.2.0，App 边收边播：字幕轨多的 MKV 不用先下完一大块索引，拖到没缓冲的位置也直接从那里开始读。续播（包括重新打开 App 后续播）也快了很多。升级服务器与 App 后自动生效。

- 换低画质的提示来得更早：连续等满 8 秒（等首帧、拖进度后、播放中卡住都算），且网速低于片子码率时就会提示。每次播放最多提示一次，选不选由你，不会自动切换。
- MKV 起播提速从第二次播放这个文件、以及追剧的下一集起生效：第一次播放时服务器才生成一份精简索引，在「设置 → 更新与维护 → 缓存管理」里显示为「MKV 精简索引」。
- App 会把最近播放的数据留在手机上，重开 App 也在，最多 512 MB，30 天没用自动删除；手机空间紧张时系统也会回收。

#### 「片段」：在自己的片库里竖滑刷片

不知道看什么时，在 iPhone App「媒体库」页右上角点「▶ 片段」，竖着一条条滑，看从你的电影和剧集里挑出的精彩片段。看中了点「详情」进条目页，或点「全屏观看」再点「看全片」接着看。可以按电影 / 剧集、类型、年代、国家 / 地区、最低评分、片长筛选，也可以只看没看过的。

- 需要 iPhone 0.2.0 和本版服务器。成员只会刷到自己能看的媒体库。
- 只支持 MKV、MP4 的电影和剧集；蓝光、DVD、ISO、TS 和网盘 strm 条目会跳过。剧集的片段取自第一季第 2 集。
- 画质在家默认原画，在外网或蜂窝默认 720p，可用画质按钮切换。用蜂窝网络进入前会先确认一次。
- 刷片段不计入观看记录；只有点「已看」或接着看全片才会记录。

#### 默认音轨、字幕自己就选对

以前默认轨跟着片源的标记走，常常放的是配音，字幕也要自己打开。现在默认音轨是影片原声，默认字幕按媒体库的元数据语言来选：中文库里的外语片会自动开中文字幕。只有你亲手换过的音轨、字幕才会被记住，自动挑的不会再沿用到整部剧。网页和 iPhone 0.2.0 升级后自动生效，Infuse 等 Jellyfin 客户端可能也会跟着生效。

- 条目页的音轨、字幕行会标出你起播时会放哪一条，并写明原因。
- 导评、口述影像这类轨不会被自动选中。
- 升级时，记忆里只是旧规则自动挑出的那些会被清掉，改按新规则选；你自己选过的保留。

### 新功能

- **片名 Logo**：网页（默认主题）和 iPhone App 的条目页改用片名 Logo 显示片名，Jellyfin 客户端也能拿到。想换一张，在网页上到条目的 ⋯ →「更换图片」→「徽标」里挑。老条目在下一次刷新元数据时补上 Logo；开了向媒体目录写入图片的库，会多写一个 `clearlogo.png`。
- **手动下载能确认作品**：搜索结果的下载认不出是哪部片时，可以在网页或 iPhone 上按片名搜索并点选正确的作品，媒体库仍和自动识别时一样自动分配。
- **iPhone 上切换账号**：长按底部头像页签切换账号，双击切回上一个账号。
- **iPhone「接着看」浮条**：iOS 26.1 及以上，标签栏上方显示下一部要接着看的片，点一下就播；长按可以暂时隐藏。

### 体验改进

- **iPhone 页签**：顺序改为「媒体库、订阅、发现、活动、我的」，所有账号打开 App 都先到「媒体库」。以前管理员先到「发现」。
- **iPhone「我的」页**：「设置」改名「服务器设置」；「关于 MovieClaw」挪到「服务器设置」页最底部，「退出登录」挪到个人信息页最底部。
- **大库筛选更快**：文件很多的媒体库，打开「更多筛选」和进入库页面都快了很多。在我们的测试中，1.8 万个文件的库从约两分钟缩短到不到一秒。
- **AI 字幕即时响应**：发起 AI 字幕即时响应，停止几秒内生效；读取字幕轨有单独的进度，模型出错时会说明原因，暂时故障后从中断处继续。
- **细节打磨**：iPhone 播放时按侧键调音量，会弹出和竖滑一样的音量提示；发现页、订阅页的大图在拉过头或横屏时不会再露出边缘底色。网页桌面端（默认主题）的活动页改为与 App、手机一致的「总览 + 二级页」。

### 修好了

- Mac 转码器使用 ffmpeg 7.1 时，需要缩小画面的显卡转码总是失败的问题已修复。
- 季文件夹名带额外文字（如 `Season 4-第 4 季-(2007)`）时会被当成单独一部片的问题已修复。季号定不下来时，剧集以前会被悄悄归进特别篇；现在进入待识别，由你指定第几季。
- 洗版之后，同一个下载有时会被再复制进媒体库一份的问题已修复。
- 网页播放时，同时有国语 DTS 和英语 AC3 音轨的片子，不会再为了省转码换成英语。
- iPhone 播放器的暗场画面正中，不会再隐约露出一个椭圆。

### 管理员须知

- MovieClaw 的页面不能再嵌入其他网站的 iframe（比如导航面板）。API 描述文件不再公开，需要时设置 `APP_ENV=local` 恢复。
- 服务器启动后第一次有人打开「片段」时，会在后台把每部电影、每部剧的一个文件的索引读一遍，只读一次。

### 升级须知

- **「生成章节」改为默认关闭，已有的媒体库也全部关掉。** 关闭期间，条目页不显示章节横排，Infuse 等 Jellyfin 客户端也拿不到章节图；文件自带的章节照常可用。要重新打开（管理员）：「媒体库 → 齿轮按钮（管理媒体库）→ 库所在行的 ⋯ → 编辑库 → 扫描与监控」里的「生成章节」。已生成的图都还在，缺的会在后台补生成。
- 网页和 iPhone 的字幕样式去掉了「描边」。不开背景时，字幕统一为白字加柔和投影。
- 本版包含数据库迁移，应用内更新会先自动备份。要回到 v0.28.0：到「设置 → 更新与维护 →『选择版本回退…』」，它会恢复这份备份，更新之后产生的数据会丢失。如果你是拉取 Docker 镜像更新的，请先停止容器并备份数据目录；回退时把镜像标签改回 `v0.28.0`，并恢复这份备份。

## v0.29.0: Faster playback away from home

The iPhone app starts and seeks much faster on slow remote connections, and offers a lower quality when bandwidth cannot keep up. Browse short Clips from your collection, with better default audio and subtitles.

> ✅ No Docker image update is required. Update from **Settings → Update & Maintenance**. Update the iPhone app to **0.2.0** as well.

### Highlights

#### Watch a 4K film on mobile without the long wait

High-bitrate originals previously took many seconds to start or seek over cellular connections. The app now plays while receiving data, avoids downloading a large index first for subtitle-heavy MKVs, and reads directly from uncached seek positions. In tests with the same 4K source on a slow connection, startup dropped to a second or two and seeking from around half a minute to a few seconds. After eight seconds of insufficient bandwidth, it offers a lower quality; you decide whether to switch. Update both the server and app for this behavior.

#### Swipe through Clips from your own collection

Select Clips at the top right of the app's Library page to browse excerpts from your movies and series vertically. Open details or continue fullscreen when something catches your eye. Filter by genre, region or rating; the server selects the excerpts automatically.

#### Better default audio and subtitles

Default audio uses the original language, and subtitles follow the library's language. Only tracks you explicitly choose are remembered, so automatic selections do not bias future playback. This applies automatically after upgrading.

### More features and improvements

- **Title logos**: Details use a title logo, with manual selection or automatic restoration in Change Artwork.
- **Faster large-library filters**: More Filters and detail pages open faster in file-heavy libraries.
- **Responsive AI subtitle controls**: Start and stop requests respond immediately and model failures explain their cause.
- **Confirm unidentified manual downloads**: Use the search term to confirm the title and route it to a library.
- **More comfortable app controls**: Hardware volume buttons work during playback; Me is reorganized, with long-press or double-tap on the avatar tab to switch accounts.

### Fixed

- Mac transcoder hardware acceleration works with newer ffmpeg versions.
- Decorated season folder names are not treated as new titles, and uncertain season numbers are not silently assigned to specials.
- Quality-upgrade files are not occasionally imported a second time for the same download.
- Corrected a faint oval visible in the center of dark playback scenes.

### Before you update

- **Chapter generation is now off by default, including for existing libraries.** It is CPU- and disk-intensive. Re-enable Generate Chapters when editing a library in Settings → Libraries if needed. Existing images are retained and immediately available again.
- Subtitle outlines are removed. Text without a background uses white letters with a soft shadow, consistently in the app and on the web.
- This release migrates the database. To return to v0.28.0 or earlier, restore the automatic pre-update backup.

Thanks to @miniers for their contributions.

## v0.29.0：在外面也能秒开

这一版主攻 iPhone App 在外网的播放：起播、拖进度条在慢网络下快了好几倍，网速跟不上时会提示换画质。
App 还多了「片段」，在片库里竖着滑就能刷片。默认音轨、字幕也更懂你了。

> ✅ 本版无需更新 Docker 镜像，在「设置 → 更新与维护」一键更新即可。iPhone App 请同时更新到 0.2.0。

### 本版亮点

#### 在外面用手机看 4K，也不用干等

以前出门用蜂窝网络看高码率原片，起播要十几秒甚至半分钟，拖一下进度又是半天。现在 App 边收边播，
字幕多的 MKV 起播不用先下完一大块索引，拖到没缓冲过的位置也直接从那里开始读：同样的 4K 片在慢网络下
从头播一两秒出画，拖进度条从半分钟缩到几秒。网速实在跟不上片子的码率时，等满 8 秒会提示你换低一档
画质，选不选由你。升级服务器与 App 后自动生效。

#### 「片段」：在片库里竖滑刷片

在 App「媒体库」页右上角点「片段」：从你自己片库的电影和剧集里挑出精彩的一段，竖着一条条滑，
看中了点「详情」或全屏接着看。能像媒体库一样按类型、地区、评分等筛选，片段由服务器自动挑选，不用设置。

#### 默认音轨、字幕更懂你

默认音轨改为影片原声，默认字幕按媒体库的语言来选；只有你亲手换过的音轨、字幕才会被记住，
不会再被自动选择的结果「带偏」。升级后自动生效。

### 其他新功能与改进

- **片名 Logo**：条目详情页改用片名 Logo，也能在「更换图片」里挑选或恢复自动。
- **大库筛选更快**：文件很多的媒体库打开「更多筛选」、进详情页都快了不少。
- **AI 字幕秒回**：发起、停止 AI 字幕都即时响应，模型出问题时直接说明原因。
- **手动下载能确认条目**：搜索页手动下载认不出是哪部片时，可按搜索词确认，再照常分到媒体库。
- **App 更顺手**：播放时按侧键调音量，「我的」页重新整理，长按或双击头像页签切换账号。

### 修好了

- 用 Mac 转码器、搭配新版 ffmpeg 时显卡转码总是失败，现在正常了。
- 季文件夹名带装饰文字时被当成一部新片、季号认不出时悄悄归进特别篇，现在都不会了。
- 洗版换成新文件后，同一个下载有时会被重复入库一次，现在不会了。
- 以及 App 播放暗场画面时正中隐约露出一个椭圆等细节修复。

### 升级须知

- **「生成章节」改为默认关闭，已有的媒体库也一并关掉**：抓章节画面很耗 CPU 和读盘。想要章节的库，
  在「设置 → 媒体库」的「编辑库」里重新打开「生成章节」即可，已生成的图都还在，打开后立刻恢复。
- 字幕的「描边」样式已去掉：不开背景时统一为白字加柔和投影，App 与网页一致。
- 本版升级了数据库。之后如需回退到 v0.28.0 或更早版本，请用更新前自动生成的备份恢复。

感谢 @miniers 的贡献。完整改动见 [v0.28.0 → v0.29.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/movieclaw/compare/v0.28.0...v0.29.0)。

## v0.35.0: Plugins, ready-cut clips and long series made easy

MovieClaw now takes plugins, so new channels and downloaders no longer wait for a release. Android TV previews and iPhone Clips can play short clips the server cuts ahead of time, and seasons with hundreds of episodes split into 50-episode ranges.

> ✅ No Docker image update is required. Update from Settings → Update & Maintenance.

### Highlights

#### Add what MovieClaw can't do yet

Adding another messaging channel or downloader used to mean waiting for a new release. Now WeChat, Telegram, Discord and Feishu channels and the qBittorrent and Transmission downloaders are six official plugins, and you can install third-party plugin packages next to them. You can also ask the AI assistant to write and install a plugin for something MovieClaw is missing. Start at Settings → **Plugins (插件)** (administrators).

- Each plugin has a detail page with what it adds, its settings and storage. A third-party package lists its permissions by risk, and you review them before it installs.
- Third-party plugins can be rolled back to their previous version or uninstalled. Official plugins can't be turned off in this version.
- Third-party plugins run in their own low-privilege process. If a new version fails to start, it goes back to the previous one. Install them only from sources you trust.
- Known issue: a third-party plugin can call another plugin's routes without the usual checks, and plugins receive the signed-in user's credentials. Fixed in v0.36.0; update to it.
- If the server crashes right after starting with third-party plugins, the next start skips them and the Plugins page offers **Exit safe mode (退出安全模式)**.
- Bound IM channels and configured downloaders keep working; channels are still set up in Settings → IM Push (IM 推送).

#### TV previews and Clips without the wait

Large previews on Android TV and Clips on iPhone used to stream the whole original file, and on iPhone away from home the server had to transcode it live. Turn on **Clip pre-cut (片段预切)** and the server cuts a short 1080p clip of each title in the background, so they play a small file instead. Start at Settings → Playback → **Clip pre-cut** (administrators); it is off by default.

- Needs Android TV 0.2.0 or the iPhone app attached to this release; older apps keep playing the original file.
- While it is on, a title without a ready clip shows only its still on Android TV and is left out of iPhone Clips.
- On Android TV, Blu-ray and DVD titles get previews too once their clip is cut.
- Cloud-drive (strm) titles, and titles too short to pick a scene from, are not cut.
- Progress shows in Activity → Tasks as **Clip pre-cut (片段预切)** and continues after a restart or update.
- Once someone uses Clips or a TV preview, administrators get a suggestion at the top of the web Library page to turn it on.

#### Find your place in a long series

Finding where you were in a season of hundreds of episodes meant a long scroll. Seasons over 50 episodes now split into 50-episode ranges with an **All episodes (全部分集)** panel, and every season opens on the episode you are up to. It works automatically on the web, iPhone, Mac, Android and Android TV.

### New features

- **Delete the download too**: When deleting a title or a file, administrators can tick **Also delete the download task and source files (同时删除下载任务和源文件)**. It is off by default and available on the web, iPhone and Android.
- **Episodes beyond TMDB**: When sites carry newer episodes than TMDB lists, the web subscription page offers to keep following them. Change the count under More → **Adjust episodes (调整集数)** on the web.
- **Search by other titles**: Searching for resources from a detail page or a subscription also uses the English and original titles, on the web and iPhone. This sends up to 3 keywords to each site, so a search makes more requests.
- **Android player and subscriptions**: Centered play controls, a Next episode button, an episode picker and an intro marker on the timeline, plus a redesigned subscription details page.
- **Mac player on top**: The window floats above others while playing. Turn it off in Window → **Float while playing (播放时置顶)**.

### Improvements

- Android switches tabs without a pause and keeps each tab's scroll position.
- The browser plugin (浏览器插件) for cookie sync is now called the browser extension (浏览器扩展), in the same place under Resource Sites; installed extensions keep working.
- Plus 5 smaller refinements: Feishu group bots can be added in IM Push without an AI model, brand red for the Netflix theme's play button and "In library" badge, mobile web Settings as a grouped list, hover polish on genre tiles, and Android TV home scrolling that may be smoother.

### Fixed

- Android TV now shows up in the app list of Xiaomi, Hisense, TCL and other TV launchers, with a proper icon and banner.
- On Android subscription details, **Pause tracking (暂停追踪)**, **Resume tracking (恢复追踪)** and the auto-renew switch work again. Marking an episode watched no longer hides the favorite heart, and new-episodes-only subscriptions no longer show "Season 1 · 0 / 0".
- The Android library home updates its totals right after you delete, restore or purge a file. The iPhone download dialog no longer says auto-import is unavailable when it first opens.
- An AI assistant conversation can continue after a reply was stopped before any text appeared, including conversations already stuck this way.
- With a naming template that uses only the original file name, scanned files are no longer renamed "未命名". Files already renamed stay as they are.
- Poster cards no longer have their top corners clipped when they lift on hover, and the site status popover in search results scrolls when many sites respond.

### For administrators

- Pre-cut clips are stored in the data directory and listed in Settings → Update & Maintenance → Cache Management as **Pre-cut clips (预切片段)**. In our tests a title took about 15 to 30 MB.
- Cutting uses the server's CPU, one clip at a time, and steps back while someone is watching. Turning the switch off stops it and asks whether to delete the clips.
- Deleting source files cannot be undone. The option is greyed out with a reason when the torrent is still used by other files, carries a hit-and-run risk, or was not added by MovieClaw, and for titles downloaded manually before this update.
- A finished series subscription can go back to tracking when sites list newer episodes than TMDB. The prompt to follow or dismiss them is only on the web.
- In `mclaw`, the `channels im …` and `channels weixin …` commands are replaced by `channels list`, `channels accounts` and `channels bindings`.

### Before you update

- This release migrates the database. To return to v0.34.0, use Settings → Update & Maintenance → **Roll back to an earlier version (回退到历史版本)**. It restores the backup taken before the update, so anything since is lost, including IM channels bound in this version. If you update by pulling the Docker image instead, no backup is made, so stop the container and back up the data directory first.
- **Adult (成人)** is no longer a built-in search category; if you had turned it on, its tab disappears. To bring it back, go to Settings → Resource Sites → Search categories → **New custom category (新建自定义分类)** and select Adult.
- A custom site YAML whose `custom_class` is not a built-in site class is now skipped, with a warning in the log.
- To get the app changes above, install the apps attached to this release: Android 0.2.0 from https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.35.0/MovieClaw-Android-arm64.apk and Android TV 0.2.0 from https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.35.0/MovieClaw-AndroidTV.apk. The iPhone and Mac apps keep version 0.7.0 but are rebuilt: https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.35.0/MovieClaw-iOS-unsigned.ipa and https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.35.0/MovieClaw-macos-arm64.zip. The Mac transcoder does not need updating.

Thanks to @WadeChenn for the Android player and subscription work, @duchanghexuhe for the Netflix theme and poster card polish, and @anlan-home for the Android TV launcher fix. Full changes: [v0.34.0 → v0.35.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.34.0...v0.35.0).

## 简体中文

## v0.35.0：装插件、预切片段、长剧好找

MovieClaw 可以装插件了，多一个通道或下载器不用再等新版本。Android TV 的预告和 iPhone 的片段可以播服务器提前切好的小片段；几百集的季按 50 集分段。

> ✅ 本版无需更新 Docker 镜像，在「设置 → 更新与维护」更新即可。

### 本版亮点

#### MovieClaw 还不会的，装个插件补上

以前想多接一个消息通道或下载器，只能等新版本。现在微信、Telegram、Discord、飞书四个通道和 qBittorrent、Transmission 两个下载器是六个官方插件，你也可以在旁边装第三方插件包。还可以让 AI 助手为缺的功能写一个插件并装好。管理员从「设置 → 插件」开始。

- 每个插件有详情页，列出它带来的功能、设置和存储；第三方插件包按风险分级列出权限，安装前要先核对。
- 第三方插件可以回到上一版或卸载；官方插件在这一版里不能停用。
- 第三方插件在独立的低权限进程里运行；新版本起不来时会自动回到上一版。请只安装可信来源的插件。
- 已知问题：第三方插件可以绕过检查直接调用另一个插件的接口，插件还能拿到当前用户的登录凭据。已在 v0.36.0 修复，升级即可。
- 如果装了第三方插件后服务器启动不久就崩溃，下次启动会跳过这些插件，插件页上有「退出安全模式」。
- 已绑定的 IM 通道和已配置的下载器照常可用；通道仍在「设置 → IM 推送」里接入。

#### 电视预告和片段，不用再等

以前 Android TV 的大图预告和 iPhone 的片段都要播完整原片，iPhone 在外网时还得让服务器实时转码。打开「片段预切」后，服务器在后台为每部片切一段 1080p 小片段，预告和片段改播小文件。管理员在「设置 → 播放 → 片段预切」开启，默认关闭。

- 需要 Android TV 0.2.0 或本版附带的 iPhone App；旧版 App 照旧播原片。
- 开启后，还没切好的片在 Android TV 上只显示剧照，在 iPhone 片段里暂不出现。
- 在 Android TV 上，蓝光、DVD 原盘切好后也有预告了。
- 网盘 strm 条目和太短挑不出片段的片不切。
- 进度在「活动 → 任务」里显示为「片段预切」，重启或更新后接着切。
- 有人用过片段或电视预告后，网页「媒体库」页顶部会提示管理员开启。

#### 几百集的剧，也能一下找到

以前在几百集的季里找看到哪一集，要划很久。现在超过 50 集的季按 50 集分段，带「全部分集」面板；每一季打开都停在你接着看的那一集。网页、iPhone、Mac、Android、Android TV 自动生效。

### 新功能

- **删片时连下载一起删**：删除影片或文件时，管理员可以勾选「同时删除下载任务和源文件」，默认不勾；网页、iPhone、Android 都有。
- **TMDB 之外的新集**：站点出现比 TMDB 更新的集时，网页订阅详情页会提示继续追；在网页的「更多 → 调整集数」里改集数。
- **用别名搜资源**：在详情页或订阅里搜资源时，同时用英文名和原名搜，网页和 iPhone 都支持；每个站点最多搜 3 个词，请求会变多。
- **Android 播放器与订阅**：播放键居中，加了「下一集」按钮、选集弹窗和进度条上的片头标记；订阅详情页重新设计。
- **Mac 播放时置顶**：播放时窗口浮在其他窗口之上；可在「窗口 → 播放时置顶」关闭。

### 体验改进

- Android 切换底部页签不再顿一下，回到页签时滚动位置还在。
- 站点 Cookie 同步用的「浏览器插件」改名为「浏览器扩展」，位置仍在「资源站点」页；已装的扩展照常可用。
- 以及 5 处细节改进：「IM 推送」里不接 AI 模型也能添加飞书群机器人、Netflix 主题的主播放键和「已入库」斜标改用品牌红、手机网页的设置首页改成分组列表、「按类型找」格子的悬停效果、Android TV 首页滚动可能更流畅。

### 修好了

- Android TV 现在会出现在小米、海信、TCL 等电视桌面的应用列表里，并换上了正式图标和横幅。
- Android 订阅详情里的「暂停追踪」「恢复追踪」和自动续订开关恢复正常；标记一集已看后，收藏的心不会再消失；只追新集的订阅不会再显示「第 1 季 · 0 / 0」。
- Android 删除、恢复或立即清理文件后，媒体库首页的数量和占用会马上更新；iPhone 下载弹窗第一次打开时，不会再误报「自动识别暂不可用」。
- AI 助手的回复在出字前被停止后，对话可以继续，以前这样卡住的对话也能继续。
- 命名模板只用「原始文件名」时，扫描入库的文件不会再被整理成「未命名」；已经改名的不会自动恢复。
- 海报卡悬停上浮时顶角不会再被裁掉；搜索结果的站点状态浮层在站点很多时可以滚动了。

### 管理员须知

- 预切片段存在数据目录里，在「设置 → 更新与维护 → 缓存管理」里显示为「预切片段」。在我们的测试中每部约 15～30 MB。
- 预切用服务器 CPU，一次切一部，有人看片时让路。关闭开关会停止预切，并询问是否删除已切的片段。
- 删除源文件不可恢复。种子还供着别的文件、有 H&R 风险或不是 MovieClaw 投递的，选项会变灰并写明原因；升级前手动下载的片子也不能用。
- 站点出现比 TMDB 更新的集时，已收齐的剧集订阅可能回到「追踪中」；继续追或忽略的提示只在网页上有。
- `mclaw` 的 `channels im …` 和 `channels weixin …` 命令改为 `channels list`、`channels accounts` 和 `channels bindings`。

### 升级须知

- 本版包含数据库迁移。要回到 v0.34.0，用「设置 → 更新与维护 → 回退到历史版本」，它会恢复更新前的备份，此后的数据会丢失，包括这一版里绑定的 IM 通道。如果你是拉取 Docker 镜像来更新的，不会自动备份，请先停止容器并备份数据目录。
- 「成人」不再是内置搜索分类，以前打开过的，这个标签会消失。要找回，到「设置 → 资源站点 → 搜索分类 → 新建自定义分类」里勾选「成人」。
- 自定义站点 YAML 里的 `custom_class` 不是内置站点类的，这个站点会被跳过，日志里有警告。
- 要用上文中各 App 的改动，请安装本版附带的 App。Android 0.2.0：https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.35.0/MovieClaw-Android-arm64.apk ；Android TV 0.2.0：https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.35.0/MovieClaw-AndroidTV.apk 。iPhone 和 Mac App 版本号仍是 0.7.0，但已重新打包：https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.35.0/MovieClaw-iOS-unsigned.ipa 、https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/releases/download/v0.35.0/MovieClaw-macos-arm64.zip 。Mac 转码器无需更新。

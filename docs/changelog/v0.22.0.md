## v0.22.0: Bring in your photos, and share a film

Photo libraries, film sharing and chapter images join MovieClaw. Switch family accounts quickly, choose who can browse each library, and connect multiple AI providers.

✅ If you already use the image for v0.19.0, no Docker image update is required. Update from **Settings → Update & Maintenance**.

### Highlights

#### Browse your photos month by month

Create a library of type Images to scan photos into a monthly gallery. Open an image to zoom, swipe, inspect shooting information or download the original. Movie, series and other-video libraries can also switch to a gallery of posters, stills and chapter images.

#### Share a film with someone who has no account

Administrators can create a share link from title details with an expiry and optional password. Visitors can watch the film or series without registering, select episodes and tracks, and keep progress in their own browser. Manage and revoke shares centrally; revocation ends access and playback.

#### Start from the scene you want

Title details show chapter images, using embedded chapters when available or generating sections by runtime otherwise. Select an image to play from that scene. Infuse and other clients can view chapters too. Enable automatic generation in library settings; jobs resume after restarting.

### More features and improvements

- **Quick account switching**: A browser can retain up to five accounts without asking for a password on each switch.
- **Choose library audiences**: Edit visibility for members; web browsing, artwork and players follow those permissions.
- **Shared favorites and watched state**: Web and Jellyfin-compatible clients use the same records. The home adds My Favorites, with history cleanup by title, library or all content.
- **Flexible AI connections**: Connect multiple providers, choose separate defaults for the assistant and subtitles, and switch models in conversations.
- **Complete viewing activity**: Live playback, history and statistics appear together; administrators can end a single playback session.
- **Separate library management**: Pending items, jobs and scan results share a management page, with cross-library recycling actions.
- **Inspect and clear caches**: Update & Maintenance → Cache Management shows disk usage and reclaimable content by directory.
- **Diagnose import problems**: Downloader tests check path mappings, group failures by cause and can hand them to AI for analysis.

### Fixed

- Infuse playback still in progress reappears in Activity after a service restart.
- Closing the mobile keyboard no longer leaves a shortened viewport; long filenames and popovers stay within the screen.
- Corrected hidden account-switch dialogs, missing lightbox thumbnails and unscrollable mobile share pages.
- Local photo and other-content libraries no longer receive inappropriate subscription path-mapping errors.
- Landscape covers are not cropped into narrow portrait strips, and local artwork in mixed folders stays with the right title.

### Before you update

- Photo libraries do not yet support HEIC/HEIF, album hierarchy or deletion inside the lightbox, and do not appear in Jellyfin-compatible clients.
- Chapter images are generated in the background with visible progress. Disable automatic generation for a library if you do not need it.

Full changes: [v0.21.0 → v0.22.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.21.0...v0.22.0).

## 简体中文

## v0.22.0：照片也能入库，好片还能分享出去

这一版带来图片库、影片分享和章节图。家人可以快速切换账号，各库能指定谁能浏览，AI 也能同时接入多家供应商。

✅ 已使用 v0.19.0 对应镜像的用户无需更新 Docker 镜像，在「设置 → 更新与维护」一键更新即可。

### 本版亮点

#### 自己的照片，按月翻着看

新建媒体库时选择「图片」，扫描后就是按月分组的相册墙。点开可缩放、滑动翻页、查看拍摄信息和下载原图；电影、剧集和其他视频库也能切成图廊，浏览海报、剧照与章节图。

#### 一条链接，把影片分享给没有账号的人

管理员在影片详情菜单点「分享」，设置有效期与可选密码。对方不用注册就能看这部电影或整部剧，支持选集、音轨与字幕；访客进度保存在自己的浏览器。管理页可集中查看和取消分享，取消后停止访问与播放。

#### 想看某一段，点章节图就能开始

影片详情展示章节图片，有内嵌章节就用原章节，没有则按时长生成。点击图片从对应画面起播，Infuse 等客户端也能查看章节；库设置可以开启自动生成，后台任务支持重启续跑。

### 其他新功能与改进

- **多账号快速切换**：一个浏览器最多保存 5 个账号，切换时不用重新输密码。
- **库可指定浏览成员**：编辑媒体库设置「可见范围」，网页、图片和播放器都按授权显示。
- **收藏与已看互通**：网页和 Jellyfin 客户端共用记录，首页新增「我的收藏」，观看历史也能按作品、库或全部清除。
- **AI 接入更灵活**：同时配置多家供应商，助手和字幕分别选默认模型，对话中也能切换模型。
- **观看活动更完整**：实时播放、历史记录、观看统计集中展示，管理员可以结束单次播放。
- **管理入口独立**：媒体库管理页集中展示待处理项、任务和扫描结论，回收站可跨库批量恢复或清理。
- **缓存能看能清**：「更新与维护 → 缓存管理」展示磁盘占用，按目录清理可回收内容。
- **入库故障更好查**：下载器测试会检查路径映射，故障按根因归组，可交给 AI 分析。

### 修好了

- 服务重启后，Infuse 仍在播放的会话会重新出现在活动页。
- 手机收起键盘后界面不再一直矮一截，长文件名和弹层也不再撑出屏幕。
- 修复手机切换账号弹窗被遮挡、灯箱缩略条不显示和分享页无法滚动的问题。
- 本地相册、其他内容库不再被误报订阅路径映射故障。
- 横版封面不再被裁成竖条，混放目录里的本地图也不再随意串到别的作品。

### 升级须知

- 图片库暂不支持 HEIC/HEIF、相册层级和灯箱内删除，也不会出现在 Jellyfin 客户端里。
- 本版会在后台生成章节图，相关任务可查看进度；不需要时可关闭对应库的自动生成。

完整改动见 [v0.21.0 → v0.22.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.21.0...v0.22.0)。

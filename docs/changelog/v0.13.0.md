## v0.13.0: Like an actor? Find what else they have been in

Explore full filmographies from actors and directors. Newly imported series show which episodes were added this time, while title and file details are easier to read.

✅ If you already use the image for v0.10.0, no Docker image update is required. Update from **Settings → Application**.

### Highlights

#### Find your next watch through an actor or director

Select a person from a movie or series detail page in discovery to browse their TMDB filmography. Titles show whether they are already in your library or subscribed, and you can open their details or subscribe.

#### Recently added means what arrived in this batch

Episode imports record their batch, so the home screen shows the seasons, episode count or consecutive range added now, rather than the series' entire inventory. Older records without batches continue to show their import time.

### More features and improvements

- **Compare files directly**: Added frame rate and color space; switch physical files to inspect their audio, subtitles and video parameters.
- **Consistent title details**: Discovery and library pages share information hierarchy, with synopsis expansion only when needed.
- **Cleaner poster information**: Home cards keep title and year, with new episodes and import time in hover details.
- **Compact track summaries**: Audio and subtitle labels are concise, with full specifications in details.
- **Accurate quality names**: 1080p is no longer called 2K, and HDR and color-space labels favor specific formats.

### Fixed

- The alphabet index highlights correctly after restoring scroll position or moving between letter groups.
- Subscribed posters no longer offer inappropriate duplicate subscription or completion actions.
- After restoring ffprobe, a manual full-library scan fills in historical video parameters.

### Before you update

- Upgrading does not automatically probe every historical video. Run a manual library scan if you need frame rate, color space or other missing parameters.
- This release migrates the database. Restore the automatic pre-update backup when rolling back across versions.

Full changes: [v0.12.0 → v0.13.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.12.0...v0.13.0).

## 简体中文

## v0.13.0：喜欢这位演员，再看看他演过什么

这一版能从演员、导演进入完整作品页。新入库的剧集会说明这次添了哪几集，影片详情与文件信息也更好读了。

✅ 已使用 v0.10.0 对应镜像的用户无需更新 Docker 镜像，在「设置 → 应用」一键更新即可。

### 本版亮点

#### 从一位演员，找到下一部想看的片

在发现页电影或剧集详情里点击演员、导演，可以浏览其 TMDB 影视履历。作品会标明是否已入库、已订阅，也能继续打开详情并发起订阅。

#### 最近添加，说清这次新来了什么

剧集入库会记录本轮批次，首页展示这次新增的季、集数或连续集号，不再把整部剧的累计库存当成刚添加的内容。旧记录没有批次信息时，仍显示入库时间。

### 其他新功能与改进

- **比较文件更直观**：详情补上帧率和色彩空间，多版本影片可切换文件查看音轨、字幕与视频参数。
- **详情页更统一**：发现页和媒体库使用一致的信息层级，简介只在超长时提供展开。
- **海报信息更清爽**：首页保留片名年份，新增集数和入库时间移到悬停信息。
- **音轨字幕更紧凑**：摘要用标签展示，完整规格在详情里查看。
- **画质标签更准确**：1080p 不再写成 2K，HDR 和色彩空间优先显示明确格式。

### 修好了

- 恢复滚动位置或跨字母区域后，字母索引高亮不再错位。
- 已订阅海报不再重复提供不适用的订阅或补齐操作。
- 恢复 ffprobe 后，主动整库扫描能补齐历史文件的视频参数。

### 升级须知

- 升级不会自动探测所有历史视频；需要帧率、色彩空间等信息时，请主动扫描一次媒体库。
- 本版升级了数据库。如需跨版本回退，请用更新前自动生成的备份恢复。

完整改动见 [v0.12.0 → v0.13.0](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/v0.12.0...v0.13.0)。

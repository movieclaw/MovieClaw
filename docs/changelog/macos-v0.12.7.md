## Mac 0.12.7: Find what you have not finished

Library home rows and poster walls can now distinguish titles you have not started from titles you started but have not finished. Opening **View All** keeps the row's watch condition and sorting.

> ✅ Works with MovieClaw Server 0.31.0 or later, the same as 0.12.6. The new **Unfinished** condition requires Server 0.47.0 or later. Download from https://movieclaw.io/en/download and replace the old app. The direct-download edition updates itself.

### Highlights

#### Continue from the titles that still have something left

Choose **Unfinished** in a library wall's sort and filter menu, or while customizing a home row, to include movies you have not finished and series with available episodes left to watch. **Unwatched** remains a separate choice for titles you have never started. A wall opened with **View All** keeps the row's watch condition and lets you clear or change it without changing the home-row setting.

Thanks to @o1xhack. Full changes: [macos-v0.12.6 → macos-v0.12.7](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/macos-v0.12.6...macos-v0.12.7).

## 简体中文

## Mac 0.12.7：找出还没看完的作品

资料库首页行和海报墙现在能区分「从没开始看」与「已经开始、但还没看完」。从首页点「查看全部」时，也会保留这一行的观看条件和排序。

> ✅ 搭配 MovieClaw 服务器 0.31.0 或更新版本即可使用，与 0.12.6 相同。新的「未看完」条件需要服务器 0.47.0 或更新版本。从 https://movieclaw.io/zh/download 下载并替换旧版 App。官网直装版会自己更新。

### 本版亮点

#### 从还有内容没看完的作品继续

在资料库海报墙的排序与筛选菜单中，或自定义首页行时选择「未看完」，会包含还没看完的电影，以及仍有在架集数没看完的剧集。「未看」仍是另一项，只包含从未开始的作品。用「查看全部」打开的海报墙会保留首页行的观看条件；可以在列表里清除或切换条件，而不会修改首页行设置。

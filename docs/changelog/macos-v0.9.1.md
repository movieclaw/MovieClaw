## Mac 0.9.1: A player window that fits

This is a small update: the player window can be made smaller without cutting off its controls, and films kept on a cloud drive play more reliably away from home.

> ✅ Works with MovieClaw Server 0.31.0 or later, the same as 0.9.0. Download from https://movieclaw.io/en/download and replace the old app.

### More features and improvements

- **Cloud-drive films away from home**: when a film that lives only on a cloud drive fails to stream straight from the drive, the app tries again through your home server. This needs Server 0.40.0 with a cloud drive plugin.
- **A smaller player window**: the window can shrink down to about 480 × 270 for the film's shape. In a narrow window the volume slider and the 10-second skip buttons hide, and the track and quality panels scroll instead of spilling out.

### Fixed

- Dragging the player window smaller during a widescreen film could cut off the controls and the title bar.

Full changes: [macos-v0.9.0 → macos-v0.9.1](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/macos-v0.9.0...macos-v0.9.1).

## 简体中文

## Mac 0.9.1：播放窗口大小随你调

这是一个小更新：播放窗口可以拖得更小，控制条也不会被切掉；放在网盘上的片子，在外面播放更稳。

> ✅ 搭配 MovieClaw 服务器 0.31.0 或更新版本即可使用，与 0.9.0 相同。从 https://movieclaw.io/zh/download 下载，替换旧版 App。

### 其他新功能与改进

- **在外面看网盘上的片子**：只在网盘上的片子直连网盘失败时，App 会改经家里的服务器转发再试一次。需要服务器 0.40.0 并装好网盘插件。
- **播放窗口可以更小**：窗口按画面比例最小能缩到约 480 × 270。窗口窄时，音量条和快进快退 10 秒按钮会收起，音轨与画质面板改为可滚动，不会溢出。

### 修好了

- 播放宽银幕电影时把窗口拖小，控制条和标题栏会被切掉的问题已修复。

完整改动：[macos-v0.9.0 → macos-v0.9.1](https://github.com/movieclaw/MovieClaw/compare/macos-v0.9.0...macos-v0.9.1)。
